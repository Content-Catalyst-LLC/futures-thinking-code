# Data Dictionary

## demographic_profiles.csv

Synthetic migration and demographic future profiles.

- `population_base`: Starting population for pathway simulation.
- `birth_rate`: Crude annualized birth-rate assumption used in stylized population balance.
- `death_rate`: Crude annualized death-rate assumption used in stylized population balance.
- `net_migration_rate`: Annualized net migration assumption.
- `aging_pressure`: Institutional pressure from older population shares, longevity, pensions, health, and care.
- `youth_opportunity_gap`: Gap between youth cohort size and education, employment, housing, and participation capacity.
- `migration_pressure`: Mobility pressure from labor demand, displacement, conflict, climate, family, and opportunity.
- `care_capacity`: Strength of childcare, eldercare, disability support, public care finance, and care workforce systems.
- `housing_pressure`: Housing affordability, crowding, urban absorption, and displacement pressure.
- `labor_adaptation`: Ability of labor systems to adapt to youth cohorts, aging, migration, automation, and regional mismatch.
- `climate_mobility_exposure`: Exposure to climate-related displacement, planned relocation, trapped populations, and receiving-community pressure.
- `social_cohesion`: Capacity for belonging, anti-discrimination, integration, civic trust, and public narrative repair.
- `gender_equity`: Gendered autonomy, care distribution, labor participation, safety, and reproductive freedom.
- `public_health_capacity`: Health-system and life-course support capacity.

## migration_demography_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test migration, population, care, housing, and social-cohesion systems.

## demographic_strategy_options.csv

Policy and institutional strategy options for care, youth opportunity, mobility governance, climate mobility, housing, reproductive health, gender equity, and belonging.

## demographic_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and priority indicators for demographic stress and mobility risk.

## care_urban_records.csv

Records describing care-system and urban absorption capacity.

## adaptive_demographic_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for population balance, care stress, mobility pressure, demographic stress, and adaptive capacity over time.
