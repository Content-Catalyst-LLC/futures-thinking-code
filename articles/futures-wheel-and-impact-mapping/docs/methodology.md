# Methodology

This companion repository treats Futures Wheel and Impact Mapping as a consequence-to-impact workflow.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Define focal changes.
2. Map first-, second-, and third-order consequences.
3. Score consequences for priority and distributional burden.
4. Analyze edge influence and feedback potential.
5. Map actors and actor leverage.
6. Score impact pathways for traceability and feasibility.
7. Score interventions for usefulness after cost and dependency penalties.
8. Link monitoring indicators to decision triggers.
9. Audit unequal exposure, adaptive capacity, voice, and burden shifting.

## Core Equations

### Consequence Priority

`priority = 0.22 × likelihood + 0.26 × severity + 0.14 × uncertainty + 0.22 × distributional_burden + 0.16 × actionability`

### Edge Influence

`edge_priority = influence_strength × (1 + feedback_potential)`

### Actor Leverage

`actor_leverage = 0.30 × decision_power + 0.25 × implementation_role + 0.20 × affectedness + 0.25 × knowledge_value`

### Impact Pathway Score

`impact_score = 0.40 × traceability + 0.30 × equity_relevance + 0.30 × implementation_feasibility`

### Intervention Usefulness

`usefulness = 0.40 × risk_reduction + 0.25 × time_to_impact + 0.20 × (1 - cost_complexity) + 0.15 × (1 - institutional_dependency)`

### Distributional Risk

`distributional_risk = 0.35 × exposure + 0.30 × (1 - adaptive_capacity) + 0.20 × (1 - decision_voice) + 0.15 × burden_shift_risk`

## Interpretation

The workflow does not predict future consequences. It makes consequence reasoning, actor-impact logic, distributional risk, and intervention traceability more transparent.
