# Data Dictionary

## desired_futures.csv

Synthetic desired-future profiles with target values across decarbonization, equity, resilience, institutional capacity, public legitimacy, and ecological integrity.

## current_baselines.csv

Current-state baseline values for each desired future.

## pathways.csv

Alternative pathways toward desired futures, scored across feasibility, institutional difficulty, transition speed, stakeholder alignment, resilience, justice, adaptability, and political friction.

## milestones.csv

Sequenced pathway milestones with dependencies, completion scores, risk scores, and owner roles.

## constraints.csv

Political, institutional, financial, legal, labor, and governance constraints with severity, reversibility, and mitigation notes.

## monitoring_triggers.csv

Adaptive review triggers that identify when pathways should shift, accelerate, pause, or revise.

## scenario_stress_tests.csv

Scenario stress-test assumptions for testing pathway robustness under uncertain future conditions.
