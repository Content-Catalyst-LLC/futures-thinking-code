# Methodology

This companion repository treats future-ready regulation as a legal-regulatory capacity involving foresight, monitoring, enforcement, rights protection, public participation, revision authority, regulatory learning, capture resistance, legal certainty, and remedy access.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate regulatory models, emerging risks, rights remedies, sandbox records, scenarios, pathways, and strategy records.
2. Score future-ready regulatory capacity and regulatory lag pressure.
3. Score emerging risk priority across velocity, severity, uncertainty, irreversibility, distributional exposure, regulatory gap, and mitigation capacity.
4. Score rights and remedy strength.
5. Score sandbox safeguard integrity.
6. Score scenarios for regulatory stress and future-ready opportunity.
7. Simulate regulatory capacity, rights protection, regulatory lag, public trust, and learning over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Future-Ready Regulatory Capacity

`capacity = 0.13 × foresight + 0.12 × monitoring + 0.12 × enforcement + 0.14 × rights + 0.10 × participation + 0.12 × revision + 0.10 × learning + 0.08 × capture_resistance + 0.05 × legal_certainty + 0.04 × remedy`

### Regulatory Lag Pressure

`lag = 0.16 × (1 - foresight) + 0.14 × (1 - monitoring) + 0.14 × (1 - revision) + 0.12 × (1 - learning) + 0.12 × (1 - enforcement) + 0.10 × (1 - rights) + 0.08 × (1 - participation) + 0.08 × (1 - capture_resistance) + 0.06 × (1 - remedy)`

### Emerging Risk Priority

`priority = 0.16 × velocity + 0.18 × severity + 0.13 × uncertainty + 0.15 × irreversibility + 0.15 × distribution + 0.15 × regulatory_gap + 0.08 × (1 - mitigation)`

### Rights and Remedy Strength

`rights = 0.14 × notice + 0.14 × explanation + 0.15 × appeal + 0.15 × audit + 0.14 × public_enforcement + 0.12 × collective_remedy + 0.08 × compensation + 0.08 × accessibility`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing regulatory futures, surfacing lag and capture risk, identifying rights gaps, and testing whether legal-regulatory systems can adapt while protecting public values.
