# Methodology

This companion repository treats futures thinking in public policy as an applied governance workflow involving policy robustness, equity, adaptability, coordination, legitimacy, implementation capacity, learning capacity, and intergenerational responsibility.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate policy, scenario, institutional capacity, risk, pathway, and strategy records.
2. Score policies by futures profile, fragility pressure, and long-term responsibility.
3. Score scenarios by stress pressure and governance opportunity.
4. Score institutional capacity for anticipatory governance.
5. Score risks by probability, severity, detection difficulty, governance gaps, equity exposure, implementation exposure, and mitigation capacity.
6. Simulate adaptive policy pathways under uncertainty.
7. Score strategy options for building public-sector foresight capacity.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Policy Futures Profile

`profile = 0.20 × robustness + 0.16 × equity + 0.18 × adaptability + 0.14 × coordination + 0.14 × legitimacy + 0.08 × implementation_capacity + 0.06 × learning_capacity + 0.04 × intergenerational_responsibility`

### Policy Fragility Pressure

`fragility = 0.20 × (1 - robustness) + 0.18 × (1 - adaptability) + 0.16 × (1 - coordination) + 0.14 × (1 - legitimacy) + 0.12 × (1 - equity) + 0.10 × (1 - learning_capacity) + 0.10 × (1 - implementation_capacity)`

### Anticipatory Governance Capacity

`capacity = 0.18 × detection + 0.18 × learning + 0.16 × coordination + 0.12 × budget_alignment + 0.12 × implementation + 0.10 × public_participation + 0.08 × data_infrastructure + 0.06 × accountability`

### Risk Priority

`risk = 0.18 × probability + 0.20 × severity + 0.14 × detection_difficulty + 0.16 × governance_gap + 0.12 × equity_exposure + 0.12 × implementation_exposure + 0.08 × (1 - mitigation_capacity)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing public policy futures, surfacing hidden assumptions, identifying fragility, and testing whether policy designs remain robust, legitimate, equitable, adaptive, and implementable across uncertainty.
