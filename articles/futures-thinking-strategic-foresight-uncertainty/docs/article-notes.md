# Futures Thinking: Strategic Foresight, Uncertainty, and Plural Futures

This folder supports the Futures Thinking pillar with reproducible examples for:

- scenario planning
- strategic readiness across multiple futures
- assumption visibility
- weak-signal tracking
- horizon scanning
- backcasting templates
- scenario robustness
- uncertainty matrices
- SQL foresight metadata

Important distinction: futures thinking is not prediction. It is structured anticipation for better present decision-making under uncertainty.
