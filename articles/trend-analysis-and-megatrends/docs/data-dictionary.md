# Data Dictionary

## trend_profiles.csv

Synthetic trend and megatrend profile dataset.

- `momentum`: Strength and persistence of the pattern.
- `structural_depth`: Degree to which the pattern changes system conditions.
- `cross_system_influence`: Degree of cross-domain effect.
- `reversibility`: Ease of slowing, redirecting, or reversing the pattern.
- `uncertainty`: Uncertainty in future direction or interpretation.
- `distributional_sensitivity`: Degree to which benefits, burdens, and harms are unevenly distributed.

## megatrend_interactions.csv

Interactions among trends and megatrends, including reinforcing, constraining, accelerating, amplifying, and enabling relationships.

## signals.csv

Emerging indicators linked to trend profiles, scored for uncertainty, impact, novelty, source type, and monitoring priority.

## indicators.csv

Monitoring indicators used to track trend movement over time.

## assumptions.csv

Assumptions used in trend interpretation, scored for confidence, exposure, reversibility, and monitoring signal.

## strategy_implications.csv

Links trend interpretation to decision areas, action options, indicators, review frequency, and decision linkage.
