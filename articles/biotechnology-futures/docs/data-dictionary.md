# Data Dictionary

## biotechnology_capabilities.csv

Synthetic capability register for biotechnology futures analysis.

- `scientific_maturity`: Evidence base, technical maturity, and reliability.
- `manufacturing_capacity`: Ability to scale production safely and consistently.
- `governance_readiness`: Regulatory, ethical, oversight, and institutional readiness.
- `public_legitimacy`: Public trust, transparency, and social acceptability.
- `equity_access`: Likelihood that benefits are equitably distributed.
- `ecological_uncertainty`: Uncertainty about ecosystem effects or biological release.
- `dual_use_risk`: Potential for beneficial capability to be misused or cause security harm.
- `community_consent`: Strength of affected-community participation and consent.

## biotechnology_risk_register.csv

Risk records covering ethics, dual use, ecological uncertainty, access inequality, bioeconomy sustainability, and surveillance governance.

## justice_indicators.csv

Justice records covering equitable access, affected voice, consent, benefit sharing, repair, harm concentration, and community governance.

## biotechnology_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative biotechnology futures.

## pathway_parameters.csv

Simulation parameters for biotechnology viability, biological risk pressure, and access capacity.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for biotechnology governance, public access, biosecurity, open science, public manufacturing, ecological monitoring, and benefit sharing.
