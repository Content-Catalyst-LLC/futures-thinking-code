# Data Dictionary

## regulatory_models.csv

Synthetic regulatory model profiles for law, regulation, and emerging futures analysis.

- `foresight_capacity`: Ability to detect emerging legal-regulatory issues through scanning, scenarios, weak signals, and expert-public review.
- `monitoring_capacity`: Ability to collect evidence on performance, harms, compliance, and changing risk conditions.
- `enforcement_capacity`: Ability to inspect, audit, sanction, require correction, and enforce public-interest obligations.
- `rights_protection`: Strength of rights protection across dignity, equality, privacy, due process, labor, environment, and social protection.
- `public_participation`: Degree to which affected publics shape regulatory definitions, risk thresholds, standards, and remedies.
- `revision_authority`: Legal and institutional authority to revise rules when evidence changes.
- `regulatory_learning`: Ability to update assumptions, standards, guidance, and enforcement priorities over time.
- `capture_resistance`: Institutional protection against industry dominance, revolving doors, information asymmetry, and regulatory capture.
- `legal_certainty`: Predictability and clarity of legal obligations.
- `remedy_access`: Ability of affected people to obtain notice, explanation, appeal, compensation, injunction, or other relief.

## emerging_risk_register.csv

Risk records for regulatory lag, rights under-enforcement, climate adaptation delay, sandbox capture, symbolic participation, and industry-led lock-in.

## rights_remedy_register.csv

Rights and remedy profiles across regulatory models.

## sandbox_governance.csv

Regulatory sandbox safeguard records.

## regulatory_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative legal-regulatory futures.

## adaptive_regulatory_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for regulatory capacity, rights protection, regulatory lag, public trust, and learning.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for building future-ready legal and regulatory capacity.
