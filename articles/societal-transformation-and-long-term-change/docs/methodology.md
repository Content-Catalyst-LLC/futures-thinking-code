# Methodology

This companion repository treats societal transformation and long-term change as interacting structural dynamics across technology, ecology, demography, economy, institutions, infrastructure, culture, and geopolitics.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate driver, signal, scenario, pressure, equity, pathway, and strategy records.
2. Score transformation drivers by structural force and governance relevance.
3. Score weak signals by priority.
4. Score scenarios by transformation depth, just-transformation capacity, and fragility.
5. Score pressure indicators by threshold breach and urgency.
6. Score equity indicators by exposure, voice, protection, repair, burden, and agency.
7. Simulate transformation pathways over time.
8. Score strategy options for public-interest transformation capacity.
9. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Driver Transformation Priority

`priority = 0.18 × intensity + 0.14 × uncertainty + 0.16 × system_reach + 0.14 × feedback + 0.14 × threshold + 0.12 × governance + 0.12 × equity`

### Transformation Depth

`depth = 0.18 × technology + 0.18 × economic_restructuring + 0.18 × ecological_stress + 0.16 × institutional_adaptability + 0.14 × social_cohesion + 0.08 × equity_protection + 0.08 × public_legitimacy`

### Just Transformation Capacity

`capacity = 0.22 × institutional_adaptability + 0.22 × equity_protection + 0.20 × public_legitimacy + 0.18 × social_cohesion + 0.10 × (1 - ecological_stress) + 0.08 × economic_restructuring`

### Fragility

`fragility = 0.26 × ecological_stress + 0.22 × (1 - institutional_adaptability) + 0.20 × (1 - social_cohesion) + 0.18 × (1 - public_legitimacy) + 0.14 × (1 - equity_protection)`

### Justice Profile

`justice = 0.20 × voice + 0.22 × protection + 0.22 × repair + 0.18 × agency + 0.18 × (1 - burden_concentration)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing transformation pathways, identifying pressures, surfacing marginalized perspectives, and testing strategy under uncertainty.
