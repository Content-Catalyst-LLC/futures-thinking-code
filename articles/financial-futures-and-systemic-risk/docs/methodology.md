# Methodology

This companion repository treats financial futures and systemic risk as a multidimensional system involving leverage liquidity household security climate exposure nonbank finance digital run risk regulation public finance consumer protection productive investment and distributional harm.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate financial profiles scenarios policy options risk indicators public-finance/climate records and stress pathways.
2. Score financial resilience and systemic risk.
3. Score financial scenarios for stress pressure and resilience opportunity.
4. Score policy options for financial stability gain and implementation risk.
5. Score early-warning indicators for systemic risk priority.
6. Score public finance and climate records for public resilience capacity.
7. Simulate financial stability systemic risk and public capacity under repeated shocks.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Financial Resilience

`resilience = 0.14 × liquidity_resilience + 0.14 × household_security + 0.14 × regulatory_strength + 0.10 × public_finance_capacity + 0.10 × consumer_protection + 0.10 × productive_investment + 0.10 × (1 - leverage) + 0.08 × (1 - climate_exposure) + 0.06 × (1 - nonbank_exposure) + 0.04 × (1 - digital_run_risk)`

### Systemic Risk

`risk = 0.16 × leverage + 0.14 × (1 - liquidity_resilience) + 0.14 × climate_exposure + 0.13 × nonbank_exposure + 0.13 × digital_run_risk + 0.12 × (1 - household_security) + 0.10 × (1 - public_finance_capacity) + 0.08 × (1 - regulatory_strength)`

### Risk Indicator Priority

`priority = 0.15 × probability_proxy + 0.18 × severity + 0.17 × contagion_potential + 0.14 × opacity + 0.14 × recovery_difficulty + 0.14 × distributional_harm + 0.08 × (1 - policy_preparedness)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing financial futures surfacing systemic risk identifying early-warning signals and testing whether financial systems remain resilient under uncertainty.
