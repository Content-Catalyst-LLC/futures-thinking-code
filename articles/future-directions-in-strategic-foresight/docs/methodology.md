# Methodology

This companion repository treats future directions in strategic foresight as a system shaped by signal detection, scenario capability, learning capacity, governance integration, adaptive flexibility, participatory legitimacy, ethical accountability, data infrastructure, AI readiness, and public legitimacy.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate institutional profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, signals, and adaptive pathways.
2. Score institutions for integrated foresight capability.
3. Score scenarios for foresight-system risk and transformation opportunity.
4. Score strategy options for capability gain and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for priority.
6. Score signals for strategic attention priority.
7. Simulate foresight capability, governance integration, learning, participation, ethical accountability, and adaptive flexibility over time.
8. Export reproducible outputs and a report.

## Core Equations

### Integrated Foresight Capability

`capability = 0.16 × signal_detection + 0.16 × scenario_capability + 0.14 × learning_capacity + 0.14 × governance_integration + 0.12 × adaptive_flexibility + 0.10 × participatory_legitimacy + 0.10 × ethical_accountability + 0.08 × data_infrastructure`

### Technical Capability

`technical = 0.35 × signal_detection + 0.35 × scenario_capability + 0.30 × data_infrastructure`

### Governance Capability

`governance = 0.45 × governance_integration + 0.30 × adaptive_flexibility + 0.25 × learning_capacity`

### Legitimacy Capability

`legitimacy = 0.40 × participatory_legitimacy + 0.35 × ethical_accountability + 0.25 × public_legitimacy`

### Capability Gap

`gap = max(0, technical - ((governance + legitimacy) / 2))`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing whether institutions are analytically capable, operationally integrated, participatory, ethical, and adaptive enough for strategic foresight to matter.
