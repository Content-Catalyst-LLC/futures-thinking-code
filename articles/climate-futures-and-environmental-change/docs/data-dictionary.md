# Data Dictionary

## climate_future_profiles.csv

Synthetic climate future profiles.

- `emissions_intensity`: Relative greenhouse gas emissions pressure.
- `adaptation_capacity`: Capacity to prepare, adjust, protect, and recover under climate stress.
- `ecosystem_stress`: Stress on biodiversity, forests, soils, freshwater, oceans, and ecological systems.
- `governance_coordination`: Ability to coordinate climate action across sectors, jurisdictions, and time horizons.
- `social_vulnerability`: Degree to which communities, households, workers, and regions are exposed with limited buffers.
- `technology_deployment`: Pace and strength of relevant climate technology deployment.
- `transition_speed`: Pace of mitigation and structural transition.
- `justice_capacity`: Strength of fairness, participation, public legitimacy, just transition, and protection for vulnerable groups.
- `residual_loss`: Damage remaining after mitigation and adaptation.

## climate_scenarios.csv

Alternative climate futures and environmental stress scenarios.

## mitigation_adaptation_strategies.csv

Strategies combining mitigation, adaptation, vulnerability reduction, ecosystem protection, governance, finance, and justice.

## climate_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and systemic climate risk indicators.

## climate_governance_records.csv

Institutional capacity records for mitigation, adaptation, public finance, monitoring, coordination, participation, and justice safeguards.

## climate_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for climate stress, social vulnerability, and adaptive capacity over time.
