# Methodology

This companion repository treats infrastructure futures as multidimensional systems involving network topology, redundancy, digital dependence, climate exposure, governance coordination, public finance, maintenance integrity, equity of access, geopolitical dependency, cascade risk, and adaptive capacity.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate infrastructure profiles, scenarios, strategy options, risk indicators, governance records, and cascade pathways.
2. Score infrastructure viability and infrastructure fragility.
3. Score scenarios for infrastructure stress and transformation opportunity.
4. Score strategies for infrastructure systems value and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for systemic infrastructure risk priority.
6. Score governance records for institutional capacity and legitimacy gap.
7. Simulate infrastructure viability, load, and adaptive capacity under repeated shocks.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Infrastructure Viability

`viability = 0.14 × (1 - centralization) + 0.18 × redundancy - 0.12 × digital_dependence - 0.16 × climate_exposure + 0.16 × coordination_quality + 0.12 × public_finance_capacity + 0.12 × maintenance_integrity + 0.10 × equity_of_access - 0.08 × geopolitical_dependency`

### Infrastructure Fragility

`fragility = 0.14 × centralization + 0.14 × (1 - redundancy) + 0.12 × digital_dependence + 0.17 × climate_exposure + 0.13 × (1 - coordination_quality) + 0.11 × (1 - public_finance_capacity) + 0.10 × (1 - maintenance_integrity) + 0.08 × (1 - equity_of_access) + 0.11 × geopolitical_dependency`

### Risk Priority

`priority = 0.14 × probability_proxy + 0.18 × severity + 0.17 × cascade_potential + 0.12 × visibility_gap + 0.14 × recovery_difficulty + 0.17 × distributional_harm + 0.08 × (1 - preparedness)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing infrastructure futures, surfacing risk, testing strategies, and assessing whether systems remain adaptive, just, resilient, maintainable, and publicly accountable under uncertainty.
