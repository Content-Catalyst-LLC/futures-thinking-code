# Data Dictionary

## urban_system_profiles.csv

Synthetic urban future profiles.

- `infrastructure_strength`: Condition and capacity of physical and social infrastructure.
- `governance_capacity`: Ability to coordinate, implement, monitor, learn, and remain accountable.
- `housing_affordability`: Degree to which housing is stable, accessible, and non-extractive.
- `climate_exposure`: Exposure to heat, flooding, water stress, air pollution, sea-level risk, and ecological stress.
- `inequality`: Spatial, economic, environmental, mobility, digital, and political inequality.
- `digital_integration`: Extent of smart systems, sensors, platforms, data infrastructure, and digital service delivery.
- `public_finance_capacity`: Ability to fund services, maintenance, adaptation, infrastructure, and social protection.
- `social_cohesion`: Trust, community capacity, shared institutions, and ability to act collectively.
- `maintenance_backlog`: Deferred repair burden and lifecycle infrastructure risk.

## urban_scenarios.csv

Alternative urban futures and stress environments.

## urban_strategy_options.csv

Strategy options for repair, services, adaptation, digital accountability, anti-displacement, informal upgrading, and governance repair.

## urban_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and systemic urban risk indicators.

## urban_governance_records.csv

Governance capacity and institutional legitimacy records.

## urban_stress_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for urban viability, system load, and adaptive capacity over time.
