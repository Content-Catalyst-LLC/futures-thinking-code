# Methodology

This companion repository treats institutional adaptation as a systems-governance problem involving learning capacity, structural flexibility, coordination, legitimacy, feedback sensitivity, resource mobility, shock responsiveness, rigidity, and intergenerational responsibility.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate institutional, risk, feedback, scenario, pathway, and strategy records.
2. Score institutional adaptive profiles and fragility pressure.
3. Score adaptation risks by probability, severity, detection difficulty, governance gaps, legitimacy exposure, implementation exposure, and mitigation capacity.
4. Score feedback systems by signal detection, interpretation, evaluation, memory, revision authority, community feedback, and public reporting.
5. Score scenarios by institutional stress pressure and adaptation opportunity.
6. Simulate institutional viability, legitimacy, and learning over time.
7. Score strategy options for institutional adaptation.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Adaptive Profile

`profile = 0.18 × learning + 0.16 × flexibility + 0.16 × coordination + 0.14 × legitimacy + 0.14 × feedback + 0.10 × resources + 0.08 × shock_response - 0.10 × rigidity + 0.04 × intergenerational_responsibility`

### Fragility Pressure

`fragility = 0.20 × rigidity + 0.16 × (1 - learning) + 0.16 × (1 - flexibility) + 0.14 × (1 - coordination) + 0.12 × (1 - legitimacy) + 0.12 × (1 - feedback) + 0.10 × (1 - resources)`

### Feedback Capacity

`feedback = 0.18 × signal_detection + 0.16 × interpretation + 0.16 × evaluation + 0.14 × memory + 0.14 × revision_authority + 0.12 × community_feedback + 0.10 × public_reporting`

### Risk Priority

`risk = 0.18 × probability + 0.20 × severity + 0.14 × detection_difficulty + 0.16 × governance_gap + 0.12 × legitimacy_exposure + 0.12 × implementation_exposure + 0.08 × (1 - mitigation_capacity)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing institutional futures, surfacing adaptation gaps, identifying fragility, and testing whether institutions can learn, coordinate, remain legitimate, and revise before crisis forces change on harsher terms.
