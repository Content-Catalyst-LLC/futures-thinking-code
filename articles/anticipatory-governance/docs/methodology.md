# Methodology

This companion repository treats anticipatory governance as a systems-governance workflow involving signal detection interpretation scenario capacity preparedness legitimacy coordination adaptive authority equity safeguards learning and implementation.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate governance profiles signals risks scenarios pathways and strategy records.
2. Score anticipatory governance capacity and fragility pressure.
3. Score weak signals by priority and response gap.
4. Score emerging risks by probability severity uncertainty detection difficulty justice exposure governance gap and mitigation capacity.
5. Score scenarios by future stress pressure and democratic anticipatory opportunity.
6. Simulate anticipatory capacity legitimacy learning and risk pressure over time.
7. Score strategy options for institutionalizing anticipatory governance.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Anticipatory Capacity

`capacity = 0.12 × detection + 0.12 × interpretation + 0.12 × scenario + 0.12 × preparedness + 0.12 × legitimacy + 0.10 × coordination + 0.10 × adaptive_authority + 0.08 × equity + 0.07 × learning + 0.05 × implementation`

### Fragility Pressure

`fragility = 0.16 × (1 - detection) + 0.14 × (1 - preparedness) + 0.14 × (1 - adaptive_authority) + 0.14 × (1 - coordination) + 0.14 × (1 - legitimacy) + 0.12 × (1 - equity) + 0.08 × (1 - learning) + 0.08 × (1 - implementation)`

### Signal Priority

`signal_priority = 0.16 × signal_strength + 0.14 × novelty + 0.12 × uncertainty + 0.18 × system_relevance + 0.16 × justice_relevance + 0.12 × detection_difficulty + 0.12 × (1 - response_readiness)`

### Risk Priority

`risk = 0.16 × probability + 0.18 × severity + 0.14 × uncertainty + 0.14 × detection_difficulty + 0.14 × justice_exposure + 0.14 × governance_gap + 0.10 × (1 - mitigation_capacity)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing anticipatory governance strategies surfacing weak-signal priorities identifying institutional fragility and testing whether public systems can detect interpret prepare act learn and remain legitimate under uncertainty.
