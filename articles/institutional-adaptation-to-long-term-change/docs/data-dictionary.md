# Data Dictionary

## institutional_profiles.csv

Synthetic institutional profile register for adaptation analysis.

- `learning_capacity`: Ability to gather evidence interpret feedback and revise practice.
- `structural_flexibility`: Ability to modify rules procedures mandates and operating routines.
- `coordination_capacity`: Ability to align agencies jurisdictions sectors and communities.
- `legitimacy`: Public trust transparency fairness and acceptance of authority.
- `feedback_sensitivity`: Ability to detect weak signals early warnings and implementation failure.
- `resource_mobility`: Ability to redirect budgets staff expertise and attention toward new needs.
- `shock_responsiveness`: Ability to act under crisis or disruption without losing accountability.
- `rigidity`: Degree of path dependency legal constraint professional closure and procedural inertia.
- `intergenerational_responsibility`: Degree to which future publics and long-term consequences are considered.

## adaptation_risk_register.csv

Risk records covering implementation gaps path dependency coordination failure regulatory lag memory decay symbolic participation and technology accountability.

## feedback_indicators.csv

Feedback records covering signal detection interpretation evaluation memory revision authority community feedback and public reporting.

## adaptation_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative institutional adaptation futures.

## adaptive_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for institutional viability legitimacy and learning over time.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for learning systems adaptive legal design cross-agency coordination participatory governance budgeting feedback accountability resource mobility and power analysis.
