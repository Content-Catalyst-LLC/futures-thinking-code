# Methodology

This companion repository treats the history of futures thinking as a structured interpretive dataset.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Compare historical traditions across methodological, institutional, participatory, ethical, and systems dimensions.
2. Identify traditions with high power risk.
3. Track institutional developments in futures studies and foresight.
4. Map method genealogy and ethical sensitivity.
5. Create a timeline of major historical periods.
6. Generate lessons for contemporary foresight practice.

## Core Equations

### Reflective Foresight Score

`reflective_score = 0.25 × methodological_discipline + 0.25 × participatory_depth + 0.25 × ethical_reflection + 0.25 × systems_orientation`

### Power Risk Score

`power_risk = institutional_power × (1 - participatory_depth) × (1 - ethical_reflection)`

### Method Risk Score

`method_risk = technocratic_risk × (1 - participatory_potential) × (1 - ethical_sensitivity)`

### Institutional Balance Score

`institutional_balance = 0.50 × method_influence + 0.50 × public_accountability`

## Interpretation

These scores are not claims of objective historical measurement. They are transparent teaching and research aids that make interpretive assumptions explicit.
