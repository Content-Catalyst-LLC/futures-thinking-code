# Ethical Use of Futures Thinking

Futures thinking should not be used to disguise ideology as inevitability, exclude affected communities, or turn uncertainty into manipulation.

## Guardrails

- Ask who defines the future.
- Distinguish possible, plausible, probable, and preferable futures.
- Make assumptions visible.
- Include marginalized perspectives and affected communities.
- Do not confuse technological possibility with public good.
- Avoid collapse fatalism and techno-solutionism.
- Preserve democratic contestability.
- Keep scenarios revisable.
