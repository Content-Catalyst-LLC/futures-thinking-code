# Data Dictionary

## energy_transition_capabilities.csv

Synthetic capability register for energy transition futures analysis.

- `clean_power_expansion`: Renewable and other low-carbon generation expansion capacity.
- `grid_readiness`: Transmission, distribution, interconnection, and system planning readiness.
- `storage_flexibility`: Storage, demand response, and flexible load capacity.
- `electrification_capacity`: Ability to electrify buildings, transport, and selected industrial uses.
- `fossil_phase_down`: Credible policy and institutional capacity for managed fossil decline.
- `energy_justice`: Affordability, community benefit, public legitimacy, participation, and harm reduction.
- `labor_transition`: Worker protection, retraining, wage security, pensions, and regional reinvestment.
- `material_responsibility`: Critical-mineral governance, circularity, substitution, labor standards, and ecological protection.
- `climate_resilience`: Ability of energy systems to withstand heat, storms, wildfire, drought, flooding, and compound shocks.

## transition_risk_register.csv

Risk records covering grid bottlenecks, public legitimacy, reliability, energy justice, transport, just transition, industrial policy, and material justice.

## justice_indicators.csv

Justice records covering affordability, community voice, worker security, health benefit, ownership access, repair capacity, and harm reduction.

## energy_transition_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative energy transition futures.

## pathway_parameters.csv

Simulation parameters for transition capacity, emissions pressure, and justice-resilience scores.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for grid investment, clean-power deployment, electrification support, fossil phase-down planning, justice investment, labor protection, material governance, resilience investment, and public finance.
