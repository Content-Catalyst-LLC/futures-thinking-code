# Data Dictionary

## strategy_profiles.csv

Synthetic strategy profiles for comparing futures-ready business strategies.

- `innovation_capacity`: Ability to develop future-relevant products, services, processes, or business models.
- `uncertainty_exposure`: Exposure to market, technology, regulatory, supply-chain, capital, ecological, or legitimacy uncertainty.
- `resilience`: Ability to absorb disruption, recover, and maintain essential value creation.
- `strategic_flexibility`: Ability to change direction, preserve options, and avoid lock-in.
- `organizational_alignment`: Alignment among leadership, incentives, culture, capabilities, and execution.
- `sensing_capability`: Ability to detect weak signals, trends, and emerging strategic issues.
- `capital_flexibility`: Ability to fund options, adaptation, resilience, and transformation under uncertainty.
- `legitimacy_trust`: Stakeholder trust, regulatory credibility, and social legitimacy.
- `transition_readiness`: Ability to adapt to climate, regulatory, technology, and market transitions.

## future_scenarios.csv

Scenario records used to stress-test strategy profiles.

## strategic_options.csv

Strategic option records describing staged investments, pilots, partnerships, and capability positions.

## capability_register.csv

Capability records for foresight, innovation, resilience, transition, governance, and execution.

## early_warning_indicators.csv

Indicators that can be monitored for scenario movement and strategic trigger decisions.

## dynamic_capability_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for strategic viability, option value, fragility, and learning over time.
