# Data Dictionary

## scenarios.csv

Synthetic scenario futures used for robustness analysis.

- `disruption_level`: Degree of shock, volatility, and disturbance.
- `public_trust`: Legitimacy and cooperation capacity.
- `fiscal_capacity`: Resource capacity for implementation.
- `implementation_capacity`: Institutional ability to deliver strategy.
- `ecological_stress`: Ecological pressure and environmental limits.
- `technology_volatility`: Pace and uncertainty of technological change.
- `distributional_pressure`: Unequal exposure and burden.

## strategies.csv

Candidate strategy portfolio.

- `baseline_effectiveness`: Expected-case effectiveness before scenario adjustment.
- `shock_absorption`: Ability to absorb disruption.
- `equity_quality`: Distributional fairness and burden protection.
- `adaptability`: Ability to revise under changing conditions.
- `implementation_complexity`: Implementation burden.
- `legitimacy_design`: Built-in accountability, participation, and trust support.
- `transformability`: Capacity to support deeper system change.

## performance_criteria.csv

Multi-criteria weighting structure.

## adaptive_triggers.csv

Indicators, thresholds, review frequencies, and decision responses.

## vulnerability_conditions.csv

Known or anticipated strategy failure modes.

## assumption_register.csv

Fragile assumptions connected to monitoring indicators and revision rules.
