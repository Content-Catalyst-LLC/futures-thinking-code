# Methodology

This companion repository treats strategic foresight methods as an integrated decision-support architecture.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Score foresight methods across method-function dimensions.
2. Evaluate institutional foresight pipelines.
3. Prioritize weak signals and emerging indicators.
4. Score drivers and critical uncertainties.
5. Identify vulnerable assumptions in the foresight process.
6. Translate foresight insights into decisions, indicators, and review cycles.

## Core Equations

### Method Profile Score

`profile = 0.16 × detection + 0.14 × ambiguity + 0.16 × structural_depth + 0.18 × actionability + 0.14 × participation + 0.10 × institutional_fit + 0.12 × learning`

### Method Risk Score

`risk = technocratic_risk × (1 - participatory_depth) × (1 - learning_value)`

### Driver Criticality

`criticality = uncertainty × impact × velocity`

### Signal Watch Score

`watch_score = 0.35 × uncertainty + 0.40 × impact + 0.25 × novelty`

### Assumption Vulnerability

`vulnerability = exposure × (1 - confidence) × (1 + (1 - reversibility))`

### Actionable Foresight

`actionable_foresight = method_gain + legitimacy + uptake - resistance`

## Interpretation

The workflow does not automate foresight judgment. It makes method functions, signals, assumptions, institutional uptake, and strategy translation more explicit and auditable.
