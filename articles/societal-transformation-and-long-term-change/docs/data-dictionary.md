# Data Dictionary

## transformation_drivers.csv

Synthetic driver register for societal transformation analysis.

- `transformative_intensity`: Estimated structural force of the driver.
- `uncertainty`: Uncertainty in direction, timing, scale, and interaction.
- `system_reach`: Breadth of impact across social systems.
- `feedback_strength`: Degree to which the driver reinforces or interacts with other drivers.
- `threshold_proximity`: How close the driver appears to critical system pressure.
- `governance_relevance`: Importance for public institutions and policy.
- `equity_relevance`: Importance for marginalized communities and distributional justice.

## weak_signals.csv

Early signals linked to transformation drivers.

## transformation_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative societal transformation pathways.

## system_pressure_indicators.csv

Pressure indicators with baselines, thresholds, owners, and interpretations.

## equity_indicators.csv

Equity and justice indicators by scenario.

## pathway_parameters.csv

Simulation parameters for structural change pathways.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for adaptation, transformation, equity, participation, and public investment.
