# Methodology

This companion repository treats systems foresight as a structured workflow for diagnosing structural pressure, identifying leverage points, interpreting signals, comparing strategy pathways, and linking monitoring triggers to adaptive governance.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Score structural pressure across system domains.
2. Score feedback-loop priority.
3. Score leverage points by structural depth, reach, feasibility, legitimacy, equity, and readiness.
4. Score signals by novelty, structural relevance, urgency, visibility, and affected voice.
5. Simulate structural pressure under alternative strategy pathways.
6. Score monitoring triggers and link them to decision responses.
7. Export reproducible tables and reports.

## Core Equations

### Structural Pressure

`pressure = 0.22 × stress + 0.16 × (1 - capacity) + 0.14 × (1 - trust) + 0.16 × interdependence + 0.14 × vulnerability + 0.09 × fragmentation + 0.09 × lock_in`

### Feedback Priority

`feedback_priority = feedback_strength × (0.40 × delay_risk + 0.30 × (1 - visibility) + 0.30 × (1 - institutional_control))`

### Leverage Score

`leverage = depth × reach × feasibility × legitimacy × equity × readiness`

### Signal Priority

`signal_priority = 0.15 × novelty + 0.30 × structural_relevance + 0.25 × urgency + 0.15 × visibility + 0.15 × affected_voice`

### Structural Change Score

`change_score = 0.30 × (1 - final_pressure) + 0.25 × final_capacity + 0.20 × final_trust + 0.25 × (1 - final_vulnerability)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not conclusions. They are structured prompts for analysis, debate, monitoring, and revision. Serious systems foresight requires evidence, institutional knowledge, community participation, and ethical judgment.
