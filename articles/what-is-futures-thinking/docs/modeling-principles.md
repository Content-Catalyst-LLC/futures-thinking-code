# Futures Modeling Principles

## 1. Do not collapse futures into one forecast
A single expected future can create strategic fragility.

## 2. Make assumptions visible
Every scenario depends on assumptions about drivers, uncertainties, and values.

## 3. Compare strategies across futures
A strategy should be tested under multiple plausible conditions.

## 4. Preserve uncertainty
Do not assign false precision to inherently uncertain futures.

## 5. Include ethics and power
Futures are not neutral. Ask whose futures are being imagined.

## 6. Track signals carefully
Weak signals are ambiguous. They require interpretation, not hype.

## 7. Keep models contestable
Futures models should support debate and learning.
