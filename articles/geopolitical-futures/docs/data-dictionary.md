# Data Dictionary

This repository uses synthetic data for reproducible geopolitical foresight demonstrations.

## Core Profile Fields

- `power_concentration`: concentration of strategic power.
- `interdependence`: degree of trade, finance, technology, resource, and infrastructure connection.
- `institutional_coordination`: institutional capacity for diplomacy, law, enforcement, and adaptation.
- `technological_competition`: intensity of competition over AI, cyber, semiconductors, satellites, data, platforms, biotechnology, and standards.
- `climate_stress`: climate-security and ecological pressure.
- `economic_vulnerability`: exposure to sanctions, debt, inflation, commodity shock, and trade disruption.
- `domestic_resilience`: internal capacity to withstand shocks, polarization, and legitimacy stress.
- `information_integrity`: reliability of information systems and resistance to manipulation.
- `resource_security`: energy, food, water, mineral, logistics, and infrastructure security.
- `crisis_communication`: diplomatic and military communication capacity under stress.
