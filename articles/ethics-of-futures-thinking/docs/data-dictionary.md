# Data Dictionary

## ethical_futures_profiles.csv

Synthetic institutional profiles for ethical futures capacity.

- `intergenerational_responsibility`: Capacity to account for future populations and long-horizon harm.
- `inclusion`: Degree of meaningful participation by affected groups.
- `accountability`: Ability to review, challenge, audit, and correct futures work.
- `risk_equity`: Attention to unequal burdens and protection.
- `transparency`: Visibility of assumptions, methods, uncertainty, and values.
- `contestability`: Ability of affected people to challenge scenarios, models, and decisions.
- `precaution`: Attention to uncertainty, irreversibility, and severe harm.
- `adaptive_learning`: Ability to revise assumptions and policies as consequences emerge.
- `epistemic_pluralism`: Recognition of multiple knowledge forms, including local, Indigenous, worker, youth, disability, and migrant knowledge.
- `public_legitimacy`: Public-interest legitimacy and trust.

## ethical_futures_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test ethical foresight practices.

## ethical_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for transparency, participation, distributional justice, intergenerational review, epistemic pluralism, precaution, accountability, contestability, and adaptive learning.

## ethical_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and priority indicators for ethical failure in futures work.

## stakeholder_distribution_paths.csv

Synthetic stakeholder burden-benefit records across scenarios, groups, and time.

## intergenerational_ethics_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for present/future benefit, risk, legitimacy, precaution, and justice-adjusted scores over time.
