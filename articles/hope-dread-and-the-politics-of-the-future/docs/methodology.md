# Methodology

This companion repository treats hope, dread, and the politics of the future as dynamic systems shaped by hope, dread, agency, trust, polarization, institutional capacity, future fatigue, narrative accountability, repair capacity, democratic imagination, false reassurance, and fear politics.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate future-emotion profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, records, and adaptive pathways.
2. Score profiles for mobilization, paralysis risk, disciplined hope, and fear-politics risk.
3. Score scenarios for constructive agency and political affect risk.
4. Score strategy options for future-emotion value and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for priority.
6. Score emotion records for disciplined hope, paralysis risk, and false-hope risk.
7. Simulate hope, dread, agency, trust, fatigue, repair, polarization, narrative accountability, mobilization, paralysis, disciplined hope, and fear-politics risk over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Mobilization

`mobilization = agency × (hope + 0.55 × dread) × trust`

Dread can contribute to mobilization only when people have agency and enough trust to believe action matters.

### Paralysis Risk

`paralysis = dread × (1 - agency) × (1 - trust)`

High dread with low agency and low trust can produce withdrawal, fatalism, avoidance, or numbness.

### Disciplined Hope

`disciplined_hope = 0.18 × hope + 0.18 × agency + 0.16 × trust + 0.16 × institutional_capacity + 0.14 × narrative_accountability + 0.12 × repair_capacity - 0.04 × future_fatigue - 0.02 × polarization`

### Fear-Politics Risk

`fear_politics = 0.24 × dread + 0.22 × polarization + 0.18 × (1 - trust) + 0.16 × (1 - agency) + 0.12 × future_fatigue + 0.08 × (1 - narrative_accountability)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not emotional laws. They are structured prompts for asking whether future narratives build democratic agency, disciplined hope, narrative accountability, and repair, or intensify paralysis, false reassurance, fatigue, and fear-based politics.
