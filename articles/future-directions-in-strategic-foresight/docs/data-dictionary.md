# Data Dictionary

## foresight_capability_profiles.csv

Synthetic institutional foresight capability profiles.

- `signal_detection`: Ability to identify emerging signals, weak indicators, shifts, shocks, and anomalies.
- `scenario_capability`: Ability to build, maintain, and use multiple plausible futures.
- `learning_capacity`: Ability to update assumptions and preserve institutional memory.
- `governance_integration`: Degree to which foresight influences authority, budget, policy, and decision review.
- `adaptive_flexibility`: Ability to shift strategy as conditions change.
- `participatory_legitimacy`: Degree to which affected communities, youth, workers, or publics shape future strategy.
- `ethical_accountability`: Capacity to evaluate justice, distributional effects, future generations, and ecological consequences.
- `data_infrastructure`: Capacity to store, update, audit, and reuse foresight data.
- `ai_readiness`: Readiness to use AI responsibly for scanning, synthesis, modeling, and decision support.
- `public_legitimacy`: Public trust, transparency, and democratic acceptability.

## foresight_system_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures for strategic foresight practice, including integrated architectures, foresight theater, dashboard illusion, AI augmentation, participation, global coordination, reactive crisis strategy, and justice-centered adaptive foresight.

## foresight_strategy_options.csv

Strategy options for building foresight capability, including signal pipelines, scenario updating, governance integration, data systems, participation, ethics, adaptive strategy, AI audit, learning capacity, and public legitimacy.

## foresight_risk_indicators.csv

Risk indicators for foresight failure modes, including foresight theater, dashboard illusion, AI-generated plausibility, reactive strategy, coordination failure, backlash, governance drift, and participation tokenism.

## foresight_signal_records.csv

Synthetic signal records for scanning and early-warning workflows.

## adaptive_strategy_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for strategic foresight capability over time.
