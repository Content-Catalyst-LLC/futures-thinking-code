# Data Dictionary

## development_futures.csv

Synthetic development future profiles.

- `growth`: Aggregate output expansion.
- `inequality`: Concentration of income, wealth, assets, and opportunity.
- `ecological_stress`: Climate, biodiversity, water, land, pollution, and resource pressure.
- `institutional_capacity`: Ability of public institutions to coordinate, regulate, provide services, and learn.
- `resilience`: Ability to absorb shocks and preserve development gains.
- `fiscal_space`: Ability to finance public investment, social protection, and transition.
- `labor_inclusion`: Quality of employment, wages, rights, bargaining power, and livelihood security.
- `public_investment`: Investment in infrastructure, health, education, care, climate adaptation, and public goods.
- `technology_diffusion`: Breadth and fairness of technology access and capability building.
- `trade_resilience`: Ability to participate in trade without brittle dependency.
- `democratic_legitimacy`: Public trust, participation, accountability, and legitimacy in development choices.

## economic_scenarios.csv

Alternative global economic-development futures.

## policy_portfolios.csv

Policy portfolios for industrial capability, social protection, debt relief, climate adaptation, technology governance, tax capacity, trade resilience, and austerity.

## shocks_and_stressors.csv

Shock and stressor records for climate, finance, trade, labor, food-energy, legitimacy, energy transition, and public health.

## institutional_capacity.csv

Institutional records associated with each development future.

## development_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for development viability, fragility, public capacity, and fiscal stress over time.
