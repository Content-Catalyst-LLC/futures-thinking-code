# Methodology

This companion repository treats colonial futures and contested imagination as dynamic systems shaped by agenda-setting power, consent quality, land exposure, external control, epistemic justice, local benefit, ecological harm, reparative capacity, sovereignty recognition, data sovereignty, and labor protection.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate future profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, extraction/voice records, and adaptive pathways.
2. Score future profiles for coloniality risk and reparative future capacity.
3. Score scenarios for coloniality pressure and decolonial opportunity.
4. Score strategy options for reparative value and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for coloniality-risk priority.
6. Score extraction/voice records for extraction burden, legitimacy, and reparative gap.
7. Simulate extraction burden, community voice, external control, ecological harm, local benefit, reparative capacity, and legitimacy over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Coloniality Risk

`risk = 0.12 × agenda_setting_power + 0.12 × (1 - consent_quality) + 0.12 × land_exposure + 0.12 × external_control + 0.11 × (1 - epistemic_justice) + 0.10 × (1 - local_benefit) + 0.10 × ecological_harm + 0.09 × (1 - reparative_capacity) + 0.07 × (1 - sovereignty_recognition) + 0.03 × (1 - data_sovereignty) + 0.02 × (1 - labor_protection)`

### Reparative Future Capacity

`repair = 0.16 × consent_quality + 0.15 × epistemic_justice + 0.14 × local_benefit + 0.16 × reparative_capacity + 0.14 × sovereignty_recognition + 0.08 × data_sovereignty + 0.07 × labor_protection + 0.05 × (1 - external_control) + 0.03 × (1 - land_exposure) + 0.02 × (1 - ecological_harm)`

### Legitimacy

`legitimacy = community_voice + local_benefit + reparative_capacity + consent_quality + sovereignty_recognition - extraction_burden - external_control - ecological_harm`

## Interpretation

Scores are not moral verdicts. They are structured prompts for making colonial power, extractive dependence, consent gaps, epistemic erasure, land exposure, and reparative capacity more visible in future-making.
