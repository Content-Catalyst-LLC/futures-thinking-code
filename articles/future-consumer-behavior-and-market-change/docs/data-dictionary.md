# Data Dictionary

## consumer_future_profiles.csv

Synthetic consumer future profiles.

- `affordability`: Ability of consumers to purchase goods and services without severe budget stress.
- `trust`: Confidence in firms platforms claims pricing data practices and institutional safeguards.
- `digital_dependence`: Degree to which consumer behavior is mediated by platforms algorithms payments and digital channels.
- `sustainability_demand`: Strength of demand for responsible low-carbon repairable ethical or transparent options.
- `access_inclusion`: Accessibility across income disability language geography age and digital access.
- `price_sensitivity`: Degree to which price changes alter consumer decisions.
- `behavioral_friction`: Defaults dark patterns overload cancellation barriers and manipulative interface pressure.
- `regulatory_pressure`: Strength of consumer protection privacy accessibility pricing platform and claims regulation.
- `privacy_confidence`: Consumer confidence that data practices are secure fair and transparent.
- `local_resilience`: Strength of local and regional market access community trust and place-based resilience.

## market_scenarios.csv

Alternative market futures for consumer behavior and market change.

## consumer_strategy_options.csv

Strategic responses for transparent value trust governance responsible AI sustainability inclusion and consumer protection.

## vulnerability_indicators.csv

Consumer vulnerability indicators across affordability trust platforms sustainability culture digital rights inclusion and extraction.

## regulatory_market_records.csv

Regulatory and public-interest market governance records.

## adoption_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for adoption trust vulnerability and market transition over time.
