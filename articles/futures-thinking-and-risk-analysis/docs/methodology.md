# Methodology

This companion repository treats futures-oriented risk analysis as a deep-uncertainty, scenario, robustness, and adaptive-governance workflow. It evaluates probability confidence, structural uncertainty, interdependence, vulnerability, resilience capacity, governance capacity, signal visibility, tail-risk severity, distributional harm, adaptive capacity, strategy robustness, maximum regret, preparedness gaps, and adaptive pathways.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate risk profiles, scenarios, strategies, indicators, governance records, and adaptive pathways.
2. Score risk profiles for futures risk and preparedness gaps.
3. Score scenarios for systemic stress and adaptive opportunity.
4. Evaluate strategy performance across multiple futures.
5. Calculate worst-case performance, average performance, maximum regret, mean regret, and robustness.
6. Score risk indicators for priority.
7. Score governance records for risk governance capacity and legitimacy gap.
8. Simulate preparedness, system stress, and adaptive capacity over time.
9. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Futures Risk Profile

`risk = 0.12 × (1 - probability_confidence) + 0.16 × structural_uncertainty + 0.14 × interdependence + 0.15 × vulnerability - 0.11 × resilience_capacity - 0.10 × governance_capacity - 0.08 × signal_visibility + 0.12 × tail_risk_severity + 0.10 × distributional_harm - 0.02 × adaptive_capacity`

### Preparedness Gap

`gap = 0.18 × (1 - resilience_capacity) + 0.18 × (1 - governance_capacity) + 0.15 × (1 - signal_visibility) + 0.15 × structural_uncertainty + 0.12 × vulnerability + 0.10 × tail_risk_severity + 0.08 × distributional_harm + 0.04 × (1 - adaptive_capacity)`

### Robustness

`robustness = 0.45 × worst_case + 0.30 × mean_performance - 0.25 × maximum_regret`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing risk futures, surfacing uncertainty, testing strategy, and assessing whether institutions are prepared, adaptive, legitimate, and resilient under deep uncertainty.
