# Methodology

This companion repository treats AI decision-making as a socio-technical system rather than a model-only problem.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate decision-system, governance, risk, equity, scenario, pathway, and strategy records.
2. Score decision-system profiles using human judgment, machine inference, coordination, transparency, uncertainty management, accountability, contestability, and equity protection.
3. Score governance controls and governance gaps.
4. Score risk severity and risk priority.
5. Score equity harm and justice capacity.
6. Simulate hybrid decision quality, uncertainty pressure, and harm risk.
7. Score strategy options for public-interest decision-system design.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Decision-System Profile

`profile = 0.16 × human_judgment + 0.16 × machine_inference + 0.16 × coordination_quality + 0.12 × transparency + 0.12 × uncertainty_management + 0.12 × accountability + 0.08 × contestability + 0.08 × equity_protection`

### Governance Readiness

`governance = 0.16 × documentation + 0.16 × auditability + 0.14 × explainability + 0.16 × human_oversight + 0.14 × appeal_rights + 0.12 × monitoring_strength + 0.12 × enforcement_capacity`

### Risk Priority

`risk = 0.22 × probability + 0.24 × severity + 0.18 × detection_difficulty + 0.18 × governance_gap + 0.12 × exposure + 0.06 × (1 - mitigation_capacity)`

### Justice Capacity

`justice = 0.18 × contestability + 0.18 × voice + 0.20 × protection + 0.18 × repair_capacity + 0.14 × (1 - error_burden) + 0.12 × (1 - vulnerability)`

### Harm Risk

`harm = 0.25 × error_burden + 0.22 × exposure + 0.22 × vulnerability + 0.16 × (1 - contestability) + 0.15 × (1 - repair_capacity)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing decision systems, surfacing governance gaps, testing uncertainty, and identifying where affected people need stronger protection and recourse.
