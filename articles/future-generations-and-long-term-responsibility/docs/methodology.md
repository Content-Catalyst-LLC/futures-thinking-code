# Methodology

This companion repository treats long-term responsibility as a system shaped by inherited burden, future freedom, institutional capacity, ecological damage, adaptive capacity, future representation, reparative continuity, public legitimacy, climate burden, debt without assets, infrastructure decay, and technological lock-in.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate intergenerational profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, inheritance records, and adaptive pathways.
2. Score profiles for inherited burden and future freedom.
3. Score scenarios for long-term risk and stewardship opportunity.
4. Score strategy options for intergenerational value and implementation readiness.
5. Score long-term risk indicators for priority.
6. Score inheritance records for stewardship capacity and burden gaps.
7. Simulate inherited burden, future freedom, institutional capacity, ecological damage, adaptive capacity, future representation, reparative continuity, legitimacy, and stewardship over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Inherited Burden

`burden = 0.18 × climate_burden + 0.14 × debt_without_assets + 0.16 × infrastructure_decay + 0.18 × ecological_damage + 0.14 × technological_lock_in + 0.10 × (1 - institutional_capacity) + 0.06 × (1 - adaptive_capacity) + 0.04 × (1 - future_representation)`

### Future Freedom

`freedom = 0.20 × institutional_capacity + 0.20 × adaptive_capacity + 0.16 × future_representation + 0.14 × (1 - technological_lock_in) + 0.12 × (1 - infrastructure_decay) + 0.10 × (1 - ecological_damage) + 0.05 × (1 - climate_burden) + 0.03 × (1 - debt_without_assets)`

### Stewardship Score

`stewardship = 0.18 × future_freedom + 0.18 × institutional_capacity + 0.18 × adaptive_capacity + 0.14 × future_representation + 0.12 × reparative_continuity + 0.10 × public_legitimacy - 0.06 × inherited_burden - 0.04 × ecological_damage`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing long-term pathways and making inherited burden, future freedom, ecological inheritance, institutional stewardship, and future-generation representation more visible.
