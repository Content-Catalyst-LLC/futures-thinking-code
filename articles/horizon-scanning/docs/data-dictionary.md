# Data Dictionary

## signals.csv

Synthetic horizon scanning signal register.

- `visibility`: How visible the signal is to mainstream institutions.
- `ambiguity`: How difficult the signal is to interpret.
- `structural_connection`: Degree of connection to broader system change.
- `domain_diversity`: Degree to which the signal crosses domains.
- `source_diversity`: Diversity of evidence sources supporting the signal.
- `assumption_challenge`: Degree to which the signal challenges existing assumptions.
- `strategic_relevance`: Estimated relevance for strategy, policy, or governance.
- `affected_groups`: Groups likely to experience the signal early or unevenly.

## sources.csv

Source audit dataset for horizon scanning. Includes source type, domain, reliability, and blind-spot risk.

## signal_clusters.csv

Clusters of related signals used to convert fragmented observations into provisional emerging issue areas.

## assumptions.csv

Assumptions used in scanning and interpretation, scored for confidence, exposure, reversibility, and monitoring signals.

## watchlist.csv

Priority watchlist linking signals to owners, review frequency, decision linkage, and trigger conditions.

## institutional_profiles.csv

Synthetic institutional scanning-system profiles used to compare scanning effectiveness under different conditions.
