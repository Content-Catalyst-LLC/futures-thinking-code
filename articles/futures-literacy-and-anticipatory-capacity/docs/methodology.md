# Methodology

This companion repository treats futures literacy as an operational capability rather than an abstract slogan.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Score anticipatory capacity across organizational profiles.
2. Identify vulnerable assumptions.
3. Prioritize weak signals and emerging indicators.
4. Examine future images across actor groups.
5. Evaluate learning cycles and decision linkage.
6. Translate insight into action options and monitoring indicators.

## Equations

### Anticipatory Capacity

`AC = Σ(w_i × capability_i)`

Capabilities include scanning, interpretation, assumption visibility, imagination range, participation, learning, and action translation.

### Assumption Vulnerability

`V = exposure × (1 - confidence) × (1 + (1 - reversibility))`

Assumptions are most vulnerable when exposure is high, confidence is low, and reversal is difficult.

### Signal Watch Score

`W = 0.35 × uncertainty + 0.40 × impact + 0.25 × novelty`

The weighting emphasizes impact while preserving attention to uncertainty and novelty.

### Future Image Risk

`FIR = assumption_risk × (1 - participation_alignment)`

A future image is more institutionally risky when assumption risk is high and participation is insufficient.

## Interpretation

The workflow is not predictive. It is designed to make future assumptions, weak signals, learning loops, and strategic translation more explicit and auditable.
