# Data Dictionary

## intergenerational_responsibility_profiles.csv

Synthetic long-term responsibility profiles.

- `climate_burden`: Future burden from emissions, heat, disaster, displacement, adaptation deficits, and climate instability.
- `debt_without_assets`: Fiscal burden that does not create durable public value or adaptive capacity.
- `infrastructure_decay`: Deferred maintenance, unsafe infrastructure, weak public systems, and long-lived spatial risk.
- `ecological_damage`: Biodiversity loss, pollution, soil degradation, water stress, and ecological inheritance.
- `institutional_capacity`: Strength of public institutions, legal systems, implementation capacity, public trust, and democratic accountability.
- `technological_lock_in`: Degree of inherited technological, AI, surveillance, vendor, data, or platform constraint.
- `adaptive_capacity`: Ability of future people and institutions to learn, respond, revise, repair, and adapt.
- `future_representation`: Degree to which future generations and youth are represented in present decision-making.
- `reparative_continuity`: Connection between long-term responsibility and repair of historical or present injustice.
- `public_legitimacy`: Public trust, transparency, accountability, and democratic support.

## future_generation_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test inherited burden, future freedom, and stewardship.

## intergenerational_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for future-generation review, climate budgeting, maintenance accounting, ecological restoration, public investment, youth participation, technology accountability, reparative finance, and adaptive governance.

## long_term_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and priority indicators for long-term responsibility failure.

## inheritance_records.csv

Synthetic records describing inherited burden, future freedom, institutional capacity, ecological damage, adaptive capacity, future representation, reparative continuity, and legitimacy.

## adaptive_long_term_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for long-term burden, future freedom, institutional capacity, ecological damage, adaptive capacity, representation, repair, legitimacy, and stewardship over time.
