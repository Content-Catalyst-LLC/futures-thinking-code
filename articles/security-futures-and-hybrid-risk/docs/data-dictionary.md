# Data Dictionary

## security_profiles.csv

Synthetic hybrid security future profiles.

- `cyber_exposure`: Exposure to digital intrusion, cyber-physical disruption, ransomware, data manipulation, and cyber-enabled coercion.
- `infrastructure_dependence`: Reliance on energy, water, health, transport, finance, telecommunications, cloud systems, satellites, ports, and logistics.
- `information_vulnerability`: Vulnerability to disinformation, synthetic media, manipulation, platform effects, and public trust erosion.
- `climate_security_stress`: Security-relevant climate pressure across heat, drought, flood, wildfire, displacement, food, water, and public health systems.
- `resource_dependence`: Exposure to food, water, energy, fertilizer, minerals, semiconductors, logistics, and other strategic supply dependencies.
- `institutional_coordination`: Capacity of agencies, sectors, and jurisdictions to coordinate response.
- `civilian_protection`: Degree to which security systems protect rights, livelihoods, health, mobility, dignity, and essential services.
- `adaptive_resilience`: Capacity to learn, recover, revise assumptions, and absorb shocks.
- `public_trust`: Public confidence in institutions, information, and crisis communication.
- `attribution_clarity`: Ability to identify actors, classify incidents, and respond proportionately.

## security_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test hybrid security capacity.

## security_strategy_options.csv

Strategy options for deterrence, cyber resilience, infrastructure redundancy, public communication, climate-security adaptation, AI oversight, resource security, and human security.

## security_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and priority indicators for hybrid security risk.

## infrastructure_dependencies.csv

Synthetic infrastructure dependency records showing cascade exposure among essential systems.

## adaptive_security_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for hybrid risk, cascade pressure, security resilience, and public trust over time.
