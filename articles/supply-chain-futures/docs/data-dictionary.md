# Data Dictionary

## supply_chain_profiles.csv

Synthetic supply chain future profiles.

- `cost_efficiency`: Direct cost efficiency of the supply chain in stable conditions.
- `supplier_diversification`: Degree to which suppliers and regions are diversified.
- `inventory_buffer`: Safety stock, strategic stockpile, or inventory slack.
- `supply_visibility`: Ability to see upstream dependencies, flows, risk, and supplier tiers.
- `labor_accountability`: Worker protections, voice, safety, and responsible sourcing strength.
- `climate_adaptation`: Preparedness for heat, storms, drought, water stress, flooding, wildfire, and infrastructure risk.
- `digital_traceability`: Data systems for origin, flow, inventory, compliance, labor, emissions, and risk.
- `circularity`: Repair, reuse, recycling, reverse logistics, and material recovery capacity.
- `regulatory_readiness`: Preparedness for due diligence, disclosure, procurement, labor, environmental, and trade rules.
- `recovery_capacity`: Ability to recover after disruption.

## supply_chain_scenarios.csv

Alternative supply chain future scenarios.

## resilience_strategies.csv

Strategic options for supplier diversification, regional resilience, climate adaptation, traceability, circularity, and public procurement.

## chokepoint_risk_register.csv

Chokepoint and dependency risk records.

## procurement_circularity_records.csv

Public procurement and circularity governance records.

## disruption_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for supply chain viability, disruption exposure, and recovery over time.
