# Methodology

This companion repository treats global governance futures as dynamic institutional systems shaped by capacity, legitimacy, legal authority, finance, collective action, technology governance, planetary risk coordination, adaptive learning, accountability, and representation.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate governance profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, institutional records, and adaptive pathways.
2. Score governance profiles for capacity and legitimacy gaps.
3. Score scenarios for global governance stress and cooperation opportunity.
4. Score strategy options for governance value and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for global governance priority.
6. Score institutional records for operational capacity and accountability gap.
7. Simulate governance capacity, system stress, legitimacy, and adaptive learning over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Governance Capacity

`capacity = 0.14 × institutional_capacity + 0.16 × legitimacy + 0.12 × legal_authority + 0.11 × finance_capacity + 0.13 × collective_action + 0.10 × technology_governance + 0.10 × planetary_risk_coordination + 0.08 × adaptive_learning + 0.08 × public_accountability + 0.08 × representation_equity`

### Legitimacy Gap

`gap = 0.18 × (1 - legitimacy) + 0.14 × (1 - representation_equity) + 0.13 × (1 - public_accountability) + 0.12 × (1 - legal_authority) + 0.11 × (1 - collective_action) + 0.10 × (1 - finance_capacity) + 0.08 × (1 - institutional_capacity) + 0.07 × (1 - technology_governance) + 0.04 × (1 - planetary_risk_coordination) + 0.03 × (1 - adaptive_learning)`

### Residual Governance Risk

`risk = systemic_stress + vulnerability + cascade_exposure - governance_capacity - legitimacy`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing governance futures, surfacing legitimacy gaps, testing cooperation strategies, and assessing whether institutions can manage shared risk without losing public accountability.
