# Methodology

The workflow treats geopolitical futures as strategic systems shaped by power, interdependence, institutions, technology, climate-security stress, economic vulnerability, domestic resilience, information integrity, resource security, and crisis communication.

## Core Scores

### Geopolitical Stability

A positive score when coordination, resilience, information integrity, resource security, and communication offset concentration, technology rivalry, climate stress, and economic vulnerability.

### Cascade Risk

A risk score when power concentration, technology rivalry, climate stress, economic vulnerability, weak institutions, weak domestic resilience, information disorder, resource insecurity, and poor crisis communication combine.

### Strategic Interaction Simulation

The simulation tracks two actor positions, escalation pressure, and system resilience over time. It is stylized and intended for scenario reasoning, not prediction.
