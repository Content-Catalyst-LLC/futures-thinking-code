# Data Dictionary

## expert_panel.csv

Synthetic expert-panel register.

- `formal_expert`: Academic, scientific, technical, or formal professional expertise.
- `practice_expert`: Implementation, institutional, technical-practice, or field experience.
- `community_expert`: Community, lived, frontline, or affected-group knowledge.
- `ethics_expert`: Rights, justice, ethics, accountability, or legitimacy expertise.
- `panel_weight`: Analytic weight assigned for demonstration purposes.
- `blind_spot_risk`: Risk that the expert vantage point misses relevant perspectives.

## delphi_responses.csv

Round-level Delphi responses.

- `likelihood`: Expert rating of likelihood or plausibility.
- `impact`: Expert rating of consequence if the issue develops.
- `urgency`: Expert rating of time-sensitivity.
- `feasibility`: Expert rating of institutional or policy feasibility.
- `confidence`: Expert confidence in the judgment.
- `low_estimate` and `high_estimate`: Expert uncertainty range.
- `rationale_code`: Link to qualitative rationale theme.

## issue_priorities.csv

Issue-level foresight priority dataset.

## qualitative_rationales.csv

Theme-coded rationales explaining why experts differ or converge.

## foresight_outputs.csv

Translation layer linking Delphi findings to policy options, scenario inputs, research priorities, and monitoring indicators.
