# Methodology

This companion repository treats future consumer behavior and market change as a multidimensional system involving affordability trust digital mediation sustainability demand access inclusion price sensitivity behavioral friction privacy confidence local resilience and regulatory pressure.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate consumer future profiles scenarios strategy options vulnerability indicators regulatory records and adoption pathways.
2. Score consumer future health and market fragility.
3. Score market scenarios for transition pressure and consumer protection opportunity.
4. Score strategy options for consumer-supporting strength and implementation risk.
5. Score vulnerability indicators for consumer harm priority.
6. Score regulatory records for public-interest market governance strength.
7. Simulate adoption trust and consumer vulnerability pathways over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Consumer Future Health

`health = 0.14 × affordability + 0.16 × trust + 0.10 × sustainability_demand + 0.16 × access_inclusion + 0.10 × (1 - price_sensitivity) + 0.12 × (1 - behavioral_friction) + 0.06 × digital_dependence + 0.06 × regulatory_pressure + 0.06 × privacy_confidence + 0.04 × local_resilience`

### Market Fragility

`fragility = 0.16 × price_sensitivity + 0.16 × behavioral_friction + 0.14 × (1 - trust) + 0.12 × (1 - access_inclusion) + 0.10 × (1 - affordability) + 0.10 × digital_dependence + 0.08 × (1 - privacy_confidence) + 0.08 × (1 - local_resilience) + 0.06 × regulatory_pressure`

### Consumer Vulnerability Priority

`priority = 0.18 × budget_pressure + 0.16 × information_asymmetry + 0.14 × digital_exclusion + 0.18 × behavioral_manipulation + 0.14 × lack_of_alternatives + 0.10 × (1 - remedy_access) + 0.10 × (1 - consumer_protection)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing consumer futures surfacing vulnerability testing market assumptions and evaluating whether consumer markets support affordability trust inclusion sustainability and fair choice.
