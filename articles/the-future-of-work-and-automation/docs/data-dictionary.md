# Data Dictionary

## occupation_profiles.csv

Synthetic occupation profiles for future-of-work analysis.

- `employment_scale`: Relative scale of employment.
- `wage_security`: Relative wage and income security.
- `task_exposure`: Exposure to automation, AI, robotics, or workflow substitution.
- `augmentation_capacity`: Potential for technology to expand worker capability.
- `worker_voice`: Ability of workers to influence technology and workplace design.
- `training_access`: Access to training, reskilling, apprenticeships, and mobility pathways.
- `social_protection`: Access to benefits, wage floors, income support, and transition protections.
- `surveillance_intensity`: Monitoring, algorithmic scoring, pace pressure, and workplace data extraction.
- `initial_job_quality`: Baseline quality of work.

## task_exposure_matrix.csv

Task-level exposure records by occupation.

## work_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative automation futures.

## algorithmic_management_risks.csv

Risk records for algorithmic management, monitoring, discipline, and automation control.

## social_protection_indicators.csv

Protection records for training access, income support, portable benefits, wage floors, appeal rights, bargaining access, and public investment.

## pathway_parameters.csv

Simulation parameters for occupation-level job quality, transition risk, and skill mobility.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy options for automation governance, worker voice, social protection, privacy, productivity sharing, and care investment.
