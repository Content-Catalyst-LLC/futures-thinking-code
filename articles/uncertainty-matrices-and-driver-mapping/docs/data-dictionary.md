# Data Dictionary

## driver_register.csv

Structured register of futures drivers.

- `impact`: Consequence for the focal strategic question.
- `uncertainty`: Uncertainty in direction, timing, scale, response, or interaction.
- `urgency`: Time sensitivity.
- `interaction_strength`: Degree to which the driver influences or is influenced by other drivers.
- `distributional_burden`: Degree to which burdens fall unevenly.
- `monitoring_feasibility`: Ability to track the driver.
- `evidence_strength`: Quality of available evidence.
- `controllability`: Degree to which institutions can influence the driver.

## driver_interactions.csv

Directed cross-impact relationships among drivers.

## signals.csv

Signal register linked to drivers.

## monitoring_indicators.csv

Indicators, thresholds, review frequency, assumption risk, trigger rules, and decision response.

## assumption_register.csv

Planning assumptions that should be monitored for failure or revision.
