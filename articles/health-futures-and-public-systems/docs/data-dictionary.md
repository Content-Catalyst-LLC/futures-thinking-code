# Data Dictionary

## health_system_profiles.csv

Synthetic health futures and public system profiles.

- `prevention_capacity`: Capacity to reduce avoidable disease burden through upstream prevention.
- `healthcare_access`: Affordability, availability, continuity, and reliability of care access.
- `public_health_infrastructure`: Surveillance, labs, vaccination systems, environmental health, communication, and emergency preparedness.
- `climate_readiness`: Ability to protect population health from heat, smoke, flood, water risk, displacement, and disease shifts.
- `workforce_resilience`: Staffing, retention, training, worker protection, and burnout resistance across health and care systems.
- `technology_governance`: Accountability, equity, privacy, auditability, safety, interoperability, and public value for digital health, AI, and biotechnology.
- `social_protection`: Income, housing, food, leave, disability, unemployment, and caregiving supports that allow people to protect health.
- `public_trust`: Institutional legitimacy, transparency, accountability, communication, and community relationships.
- `equity_capacity`: Ability to reduce unequal exposure, access gaps, discrimination, and distributional harm.
- `care_capacity`: Elder care, disability support, home care, long-term care, caregiver support, and care-worker sustainability.

## health_futures_scenarios.csv

Alternative health futures and stress environments.

## health_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for prevention, access, public health renewal, climate health, workforce, care, technology governance, social protection, trust, and equity.

## health_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and systemic health risk indicators.

## health_governance_records.csv

Institutional capacity records for public health and healthcare governance.

## health_stress_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for public health resilience, system stress, and adaptive capacity over time.
