# Data Dictionary

## participation_models.csv

Synthetic participation-model profiles for democratic futures analysis.

- `inclusion`: Ability to include affected publics and reduce exclusion.
- `deliberative_quality`: Quality of evidence, facilitation, time, reflection, and tradeoff reasoning.
- `representation`: Degree to which affected publics and future-impact groups are represented.
- `institutional_uptake`: Degree to which participation changes decisions.
- `accountability`: Public response, tracking, reporting, and answerability.
- `justice_safeguards`: Protection against unequal harm, extractive participation, and exclusion.
- `public_learning`: Ability to build shared understanding and institutional learning.
- `decision_influence`: Direct influence over policy, budget, regulation, or implementation.
- `accessibility`: Accessibility across language, disability, time, digital access, location, and trust barriers.
- `community_authority`: Degree to which communities share agenda-setting and decision power.

## representation_profiles.csv

Representation records for affected groups and future-impact constituencies.

## decision_uptake_register.csv

Records describing whether participation has response duties, budget links, policy influence, regulatory influence, implementation tracking, public reporting, and remedy access.

## future_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative democratic futures and public participation pathways.

## democratic_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for democratic capacity, public trust, institutional uptake, learning, and tokenism risk over time.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for building democratic futures and public participation capacity.
