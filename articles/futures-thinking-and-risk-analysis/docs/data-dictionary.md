# Data Dictionary

## risk_profiles.csv

Synthetic futures-oriented risk profiles.

- `probability_confidence`: Confidence that probability estimates are credible, stable, and evidence-based.
- `structural_uncertainty`: Uncertainty about model structure, causal pathways, thresholds, feedback loops, and system boundaries.
- `interdependence`: Degree of connection across systems, sectors, institutions, geographies, and populations.
- `vulnerability`: Conditions that make exposed systems more likely to suffer harm.
- `resilience_capacity`: Capacity to absorb disruption while preserving core functions.
- `governance_capacity`: Capacity to coordinate, finance, communicate, and adapt risk response.
- `signal_visibility`: Strength and timeliness of early warning signals.
- `tail_risk_severity`: Severity of low-probability or poorly estimated extreme outcomes.
- `distributional_harm`: Degree to which harms are unequal or concentrated among vulnerable groups.
- `adaptive_capacity`: Ability to monitor, learn, revise strategy, and preserve options.

## risk_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test assumptions and strategies.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy options with performance across scenarios and implementation characteristics.

## risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning indicators for systemic futures risk.

## governance_records.csv

Institutional capacity records for risk governance and public legitimacy.

## adaptive_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for preparedness, system stress, adaptive capacity, and risk trajectory over time.
