# Methodology

This companion repository treats backcasting as adaptive pathway design from a desired future to present action.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Define target futures and baseline conditions.
2. Calculate strategic gaps.
3. Compare alternative transition pathways.
4. Evaluate milestones, dependencies, risks, and owner roles.
5. Score constraints and political-economy friction.
6. Stress-test pathways against plausible scenarios.
7. Define monitoring triggers for adaptive revision.

## Core Equations

### Strategic Gap

`gap = target_value - current_value`

### Future Gap Score

`gap_score = mean(target_dimensions - baseline_dimensions)`

### Pathway Viability

`viability = 0.18 × feasibility - 0.16 × institutional_difficulty + 0.14 × transition_speed + 0.18 × stakeholder_alignment + 0.16 × resilience + 0.12 × justice + 0.14 × adaptability - 0.12 × political_friction`

### Milestone Risk Priority

`milestone_risk_priority = risk_score × (1 - completion_score)`

### Constraint Priority

`constraint_priority = impact × severity × (1 + (1 - reversibility))`

### Stress Burden

`stress_burden = 0.22 × climate_stress + 0.20 × political_resistance + 0.20 × funding_constraint + 0.18 × technology_uncertainty + 0.20 × (1 - public_trust)`

## Interpretation

The workflow does not predict transformation. It makes pathway assumptions, strategic gaps, milestone risks, constraints, and adaptive triggers explicit enough to be reviewed and revised.
