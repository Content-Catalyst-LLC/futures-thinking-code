# Data Dictionary

## decision_system_profiles.csv

Synthetic decision-system profiles for comparing human-centered, AI-augmented, automated, high-governance, public-interest, and vendor-mediated systems.

- `human_judgment`: Contextual, ethical, strategic, professional, and institutional judgment capacity.
- `machine_inference`: Model-based classification, prediction, ranking, generation, and optimization capacity.
- `coordination_quality`: Quality of human-machine interaction, workflow design, escalation, and role clarity.
- `transparency`: System visibility, explanation, and documentation.
- `uncertainty_management`: Ability to handle drift, rare events, ambiguity, and changing conditions.
- `accountability`: Clarity of responsibility for design, deployment, use, review, and correction.
- `contestability`: Ability of affected people or internal reviewers to challenge decisions.
- `equity_protection`: Protection against unequal error burden, exclusion, discrimination, and harm concentration.
- `automation_intensity`: Degree to which the system acts, ranks, allocates, or triggers decisions automatically.

## strategy_options.csv
