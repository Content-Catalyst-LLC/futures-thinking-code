# Data Dictionary

## governance_profiles.csv

Synthetic governance strategy profile register for anticipatory governance analysis.

- `detection_capacity`: Ability to detect weak signals trends early warnings and emerging risks.
- `interpretation_capacity`: Ability to make sense of signals across systems.
- `scenario_capacity`: Ability to explore multiple plausible futures and stress test assumptions.
- `preparedness_capacity`: Ability to act before crisis through plans resources and operational readiness.
- `legitimacy`: Public trust transparency democratic contestability and acceptance.
- `coordination_capacity`: Ability to align agencies sectors jurisdictions and communities.
- `adaptive_authority`: Legal and administrative authority to revise policy as conditions change.
- `equity_safeguards`: Distributional protection participation remedy and rights safeguards.
- `learning_capacity`: Ability to update assumptions using feedback and evaluation.
- `implementation_connection`: Connection to budgets regulation procurement staffing and delivery.

## weak_signal_register.csv

Weak-signal records for early warning and horizon scanning.

## emerging_risk_register.csv

Risk records covering preparedness decay regulatory lag symbolic participation ignored warnings experimentation risk transfer deferred investment and securitized anticipation.

## scenario_profiles.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative anticipatory governance futures.

## adaptive_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for anticipatory capacity legitimacy learning and risk pressure over time.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for building public-sector anticipatory governance capacity.
