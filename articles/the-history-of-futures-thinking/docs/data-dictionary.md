# Data Dictionary

## historical_traditions.csv

Synthetic comparative dataset for major historical traditions in futures thinking.

- `methodological_discipline`: Relative degree of systematic method.
- `institutional_power`: Relative connection to powerful institutions.
- `participatory_depth`: Degree of public or plural participation.
- `ethical_reflection`: Degree of explicit ethical and power awareness.
- `systems_orientation`: Degree of attention to interdependence, feedback, and complexity.

## institutional_developments.csv

Major institutional developments used to track how futures thinking became organized through journals, institutes, professional networks, corporations, public-sector units, and international institutions.

## method_genealogy.csv

Method origins and risks, linking common futures methods to historical traditions.

## timeline_periods.csv

Broad historical periods used to map changes in future imagination and foresight practice.

## historical_risks.csv

Recurring risks in the history of futures thinking, including technocracy, colonial progress narratives, tokenism, and predictive automation bias.
