# Data Dictionary

## future_emotion_profiles.csv

Synthetic future-emotion profiles for hope, dread, and the politics of the future.

- `hope`: Future-oriented possibility, motivation, and confidence that action can matter.
- `dread`: Future-oriented fear, warning, anxiety, or danger perception.
- `agency`: Perceived and institutional capacity to act collectively.
- `trust`: Public trust in institutions, knowledge, and shared action.
- `polarization`: Social and political fragmentation that can amplify fear politics.
- `institutional_capacity`: Ability of institutions to translate concern into action.
- `future_fatigue`: Exhaustion from continuous crisis, risk communication, and weak action pathways.
- `narrative_accountability`: Whether future narratives are answerable to evidence, justice, affected communities, and material consequences.
- `repair_capacity`: Ability to connect hope to historical repair, ecological restoration, and structural change.
- `democratic_imagination`: Capacity to imagine shared futures through participation, rights, and plural public life.

## future_politics_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test hope, dread, fear politics, false reassurance, participation, repair, and learning capacity.

## future_emotion_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for public agency, trust repair, narrative accountability, participatory foresight, climate truth with action, youth representation, media literacy, repair, and resistance to fear politics.

## future_emotion_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and priority indicators for paralysis, false reassurance, fear politics, future fatigue, marketed hope, youth burnout, and backlash.

## future_emotion_records.csv

Synthetic records describing hope, dread, agency, trust, future fatigue, repair capacity, polarization, and narrative accountability.

## adaptive_future_emotion_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for hope, dread, agency, trust, future fatigue, repair capacity, polarization, narrative accountability, mobilization, paralysis, disciplined hope, and fear-politics risk over time.
