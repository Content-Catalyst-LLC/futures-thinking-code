# Data Dictionary

## infrastructure_system_profiles.csv

Synthetic infrastructure future profiles.

- `centralization`: Concentration of flow, control, assets, and system dependency.
- `redundancy`: Backup pathways, spare capacity, alternate routes, and reserve options.
- `digital_dependence`: Reliance on digital control systems, platforms, cloud services, automation, and data layers.
- `climate_exposure`: Exposure to heat, flood, drought, wildfire, sea-level rise, and compound environmental stress.
- `coordination_quality`: Institutional capacity to coordinate across agencies, jurisdictions, assets, and time horizons.
- `public_finance_capacity`: Capacity to fund repair, adaptation, redundancy, access, and public-value infrastructure.
- `maintenance_integrity`: Lifecycle maintenance, inspection, repair discipline, and asset stewardship.
- `equity_of_access`: Fair access, affordability, restoration priority, and distributional protection.
- `geopolitical_dependency`: Exposure to chokepoints, foreign-controlled standards, cables, minerals, ports, fuels, or platforms.

## infrastructure_scenarios.csv

Alternative infrastructure futures and stress environments.

## infrastructure_strategy_options.csv

Strategy options for redundancy, maintenance, climate adaptation, digital accountability, governance, finance, equity, and geopolitical resilience.

## infrastructure_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and systemic infrastructure risk indicators.

## infrastructure_governance_records.csv

Institutional capacity and legitimacy records.

## infrastructure_cascade_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for infrastructure viability, system load, and adaptive capacity over time.
