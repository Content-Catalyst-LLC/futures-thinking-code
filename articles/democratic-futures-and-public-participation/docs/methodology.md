# Methodology

This companion repository treats democratic futures as a public participation system involving inclusion, deliberative quality, representation, institutional uptake, accountability, justice safeguards, public learning, decision influence, accessibility, and community authority.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate participation, representation, uptake, scenario, pathway, and strategy records.
2. Score democratic futures capacity and tokenism risk.
3. Score representation quality for affected groups.
4. Score decision uptake and public accountability.
5. Score future scenarios for democratic opportunity and participation stress.
6. Simulate democratic capacity, public trust, institutional uptake, learning, and tokenism risk over time.
7. Score strategies for citizen assemblies, community-led foresight, participatory budgeting, technology assessment, co-governance, and accountability.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Democratic Futures Capacity

`capacity = 0.11 × inclusion + 0.12 × deliberation + 0.11 × representation + 0.14 × uptake + 0.12 × accountability + 0.12 × justice + 0.08 × learning + 0.10 × influence + 0.05 × accessibility + 0.05 × community_authority`

### Tokenism Risk

`tokenism = 0.18 × (1 - uptake) + 0.16 × (1 - influence) + 0.14 × (1 - accountability) + 0.14 × (1 - community_authority) + 0.12 × (1 - justice) + 0.10 × (1 - representation) + 0.08 × (1 - deliberation) + 0.08 × (1 - inclusion)`

### Representation Quality

`representation = 0.20 × affectedness + 0.20 × representation_quality + 0.16 × barrier_reduction + 0.12 × compensation + 0.14 × decision_access + 0.08 × trust_condition + 0.10 × knowledge_recognition`

### Decision Uptake

`uptake = 0.18 × response_duty + 0.16 × budget_connection + 0.16 × policy_influence + 0.14 × regulatory_influence + 0.14 × implementation_tracking + 0.12 × public_reporting + 0.10 × remedy_access`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing democratic futures practices, surfacing tokenism risk, evaluating representation quality, and testing whether participation changes decisions.
