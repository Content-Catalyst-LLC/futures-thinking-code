# Data Dictionary

## weak_signals.csv

Synthetic weak-signal register.

- `visibility`: How visible the signal is to mainstream institutions.
- `ambiguity`: How difficult the signal is to interpret.
- `systemic_connection`: Degree of connection to broader system change.
- `propagation_potential`: Likelihood that the signal can spread, scale, or institutionalize.
- `institutional_recognition`: Degree to which formal institutions currently notice the signal.
- `distributional_relevance`: Degree to which the signal affects exposed or marginalized groups.
- `monitoring_urgency`: Need for continued monitoring.
- `affected_groups`: Groups likely to experience or notice the signal early.

## early_indicators.csv

Tracks the transition from weak signal to early indicator using evidence count, repetition, clarity, measurement quality, policy attention, and review frequency.

## signal_clusters.csv

Clusters related weak signals into possible emerging issue areas.

## propagation_pathways.csv

Models how signals may scale through adoption, feedback, recognition, legitimacy, and friction.

## assumptions.csv

Assumptions used in weak signal interpretation, scored for confidence, exposure, reversibility, and monitoring signals.

## watchlist.csv

Priority monitoring table linking signals to owners, review frequency, decision linkage, and trigger conditions.
