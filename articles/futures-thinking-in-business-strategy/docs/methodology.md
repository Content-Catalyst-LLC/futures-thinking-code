# Methodology

This companion repository treats futures thinking in business strategy as an integrated capability involving strategic sensing, scenario reasoning, dynamic capabilities, resilience, innovation, strategic optionality, legitimacy, capital flexibility, and transition readiness.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate strategy profiles, scenarios, strategic options, capabilities, indicators, and dynamic capability pathways.
2. Score business futures readiness and strategic fragility.
3. Score strategic options by net option value and scalability.
4. Score capabilities by future capability gap and investment priority.
5. Score early warning indicators by trigger urgency.
6. Stress-test strategy profiles across scenarios.
7. Simulate strategic viability, strategic option value, fragility, and learning over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Business Futures Readiness

`readiness = 0.14 × innovation - 0.10 × uncertainty_exposure + 0.15 × resilience + 0.14 × flexibility + 0.10 × alignment + 0.12 × sensing + 0.08 × capital + 0.09 × legitimacy + 0.08 × transition`

### Strategic Fragility

`fragility = 0.18 × uncertainty_exposure + 0.14 × (1 - resilience) + 0.14 × (1 - flexibility) + 0.13 × (1 - sensing) + 0.11 × (1 - capital) + 0.10 × (1 - alignment) + 0.10 × (1 - legitimacy) + 0.10 × (1 - transition)`

### Strategic Option Value

`option_value = 0.22 × learning + 0.20 × upside + 0.15 × reversibility + 0.15 × scalability + 0.14 × strategic_fit + 0.14 × signal_sensitivity - 0.20 × cost`

### Capability Investment Priority

`priority = 0.34 × future_importance + 0.20 × development_difficulty + 0.18 × coordination_requirement + 0.14 × investment_need + 0.14 × (1 - current_strength)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing strategy options, surfacing fragility, evaluating capability gaps, and testing whether business strategy remains viable across multiple futures.
