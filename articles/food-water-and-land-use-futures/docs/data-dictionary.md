# Data Dictionary

## food_water_land_profiles.csv

Synthetic food-water-land future profiles.

- `production_capacity`: Food production capacity under expected conditions.
- `water_security`: Availability, quality, reliability, affordability, and ecological-flow protection.
- `soil_health`: Soil organic matter, structure, nutrient cycling, water retention, and erosion resistance.
- `biodiversity_integrity`: Pollination, pest regulation, habitat connectivity, ecosystem function, and ecological resilience.
- `governance_capacity`: Ability to coordinate food, water, land, climate, rights, and public institutions.
- `climate_exposure`: Exposure to heat, drought, flood, wildfire, salinity, pests, and weather volatility.
- `market_vulnerability`: Exposure to food-price volatility, input costs, trade disruption, and concentrated market power.
- `justice_capacity`: Strength of rights, access, participation, anti-dispossession safeguards, and vulnerable-group protection.
- `livelihood_resilience`: Farmer, pastoralist, fisher, rural worker, and food-worker viability under stress.

## resource_scenarios.csv

Alternative food-water-land futures and stress environments.

## adaptation_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for regeneration, water governance, soil recovery, food security, land rights, technology governance, watershed restoration, and ecological repair.

## resource_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and systemic resource risk indicators.

## resource_governance_records.csv

Institutional capacity records for land, water, food, soil, biodiversity, participation, and finance.

## resource_stress_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for food-water-land resilience, resource stress, and adaptive capacity over time.
