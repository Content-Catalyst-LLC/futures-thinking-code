# Data Dictionary

## colonial_future_profiles.csv

Synthetic colonial and contested future-making profiles.

- `agenda_setting_power`: How strongly dominant institutions define the future problem, scenario frame, and evaluation criteria.
- `consent_quality`: Whether affected communities and rights holders can define, modify, or refuse a future-making project.
- `land_exposure`: Exposure of land, water, housing, territory, sacred sites, or ecological relations to future-making projects.
- `external_control`: Degree to which decisions, finance, ownership, technology, or governance are controlled outside affected communities.
- `epistemic_justice`: Recognition of local, Indigenous, worker, migrant, oral, historical, and community knowledge as authoritative.
- `local_benefit`: Share of benefits remaining with affected communities.
- `ecological_harm`: Environmental harm, degradation, or long-term ecological burden.
- `reparative_capacity`: Capacity for land return, restitution, restoration, debt justice, historical repair, institutional change, and structural accountability.
- `sovereignty_recognition`: Recognition of sovereignty, treaty obligations, land rights, or self-determination.
- `data_sovereignty`: Community authority over data, digital infrastructure, AI systems, and classification.
- `labor_protection`: Protection of workers whose labor makes future systems possible.

## colonial_future_scenarios.csv

Alternative futures used to stress-test coloniality, future-making power, extraction, and repair.

## reparative_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for land return, consent, community ownership, epistemic justice, data sovereignty, labor rights, ecological restoration, reparative finance, accountability, and legitimacy.

## coloniality_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and priority indicators for colonial futures risk.

## extraction_voice_records.csv

Synthetic records describing extraction burden, community voice, external control, ecological harm, local benefit, reparative capacity, consent, and sovereignty.

## adaptive_colonial_future_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for extraction, voice, external control, ecological harm, local benefit, reparative capacity, consent quality, sovereignty recognition, and legitimacy over time.
