# Methodology

This companion repository treats digital platform futures as a systems-governance problem involving platform power, data advantage, gatekeeping, lock-in, interoperability, public accountability, user rights, labor protections, ecological responsibility, and digital public value.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate platform, risk, accountability, scenario, pathway, and strategy records.
2. Score platform power, dependency pressure, and public-interest platform capacity.
3. Score platform risks by probability, severity, detection difficulty, accountability gaps, dependency exposure, public-harm relevance, and mitigation capacity.
4. Score accountability capacity and accountability gaps.
5. Score scenarios by public-interest platform capacity and dependency pressure.
6. Simulate platform dependency pressure, accountability capacity, and digital public value over time.
7. Score strategy options for public-interest platform governance.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Public-Interest Platform Capacity

`capacity = 0.18 × interoperability + 0.18 × public_accountability + 0.16 × user_rights + 0.14 × worker_protection + 0.14 × digital_public_value + 0.10 × ecological_responsibility + 0.05 × (1 - platform_power) + 0.05 × (1 - data_advantage)`

### Platform Dependency Pressure

`pressure = 0.24 × platform_power + 0.20 × data_advantage + 0.18 × (1 - interoperability) + 0.14 × (1 - user_rights) + 0.14 × (1 - public_accountability) + 0.10 × (1 - worker_protection)`

### Platform Risk Priority

`risk = 0.18 × probability + 0.20 × severity + 0.16 × detection_difficulty + 0.16 × accountability_gap + 0.14 × dependency_exposure + 0.10 × public_harm_relevance + 0.06 × (1 - mitigation_capacity)`

### Accountability Capacity

`accountability = 0.18 × transparency + 0.16 × auditability + 0.18 × contestability + 0.18 × enforceability + 0.10 × researcher_access + 0.12 × remedy_capacity + 0.08 × public_participation`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing platform futures, surfacing dependency, identifying accountability gaps, and testing whether platform systems are aligned with public value rather than only growth, engagement, or market power.
