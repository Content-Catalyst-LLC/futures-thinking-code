# Data Dictionary

## candidate_futures.csv

Candidate futures scored across plausibility, probability, preference, and participation dimensions.

- `driver_support`: Evidence that current drivers support this future.
- `pathway_coherence`: Internal credibility of the pathway.
- `constraint_fit`: Fit with known constraints.
- `current_trend_strength`: Strength of current trend evidence.
- `justice_value`: Distributional and ethical value.
- `sustainability_value`: Ecological and long-term value.
- `resilience_value`: Adaptive and robust-system value.
- `legitimacy_value`: Public accountability and trust value.
- `participation_need`: Degree to which meaningful participation is needed.

## strategies.csv

Strategy options scored for fit with probable, plausible, and preferable futures.

## category_shifts.csv

Examples of futures shifting categories over time as signals accumulate.

## preference_criteria.csv

Value weights used for preference scoring.

## driver_notes.csv

Driver scores used to contextualize plausibility and category-shift analysis.
