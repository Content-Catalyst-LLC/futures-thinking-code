# Methodology

This companion repository treats planetary boundaries and future pathways as coupled Earth-system and social-system trajectories. It evaluates boundary pressure, social foundations, governance, justice, technology dependence, regeneration, risk priority, and adaptive capacity.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate planetary pathway profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, governance records, and simulations.
2. Score total boundary pressure and safe-and-just pathway performance.
3. Score scenarios for planetary stress and transformation opportunity.
4. Score strategies for pathway value and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for systemic planetary risk priority.
6. Score governance records for planetary governance capacity and legitimacy gap.
7. Simulate pathway viability, boundary pressure, and adaptive capacity under repeated shocks.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Total Boundary Pressure

`pressure = 0.16 × climate_pressure + 0.16 × biosphere_pressure + 0.12 × land_pressure + 0.12 × freshwater_pressure + 0.10 × nutrient_pressure + 0.10 × ocean_pressure + 0.08 × aerosol_pressure + 0.10 × novel_entity_pressure + 0.06 × technology_dependence`

### Safe-and-Just Pathway Score

`score = 0.22 × social_foundation_security + 0.20 × governance_capacity + 0.20 × justice_capacity + 0.14 × regeneration_capacity - 0.20 × pressure + 0.04 × (1 - technology_dependence)`

### Risk Priority

`priority = 0.14 × probability_proxy + 0.18 × severity + 0.17 × cascade_potential + 0.12 × visibility_gap + 0.14 × recovery_difficulty + 0.17 × distributional_harm + 0.08 × (1 - preparedness)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing planetary futures, surfacing risk, testing strategies, and assessing whether development pathways remain safe, just, resilient, and governable under Earth-system constraints.
