# Methodology

This companion repository treats Causal Layered Analysis as a structured interpretive and reframing workflow.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Score issues for CLA depth and strategic relevance.
2. Code statements across litany, social causes, worldview, and myth/metaphor.
3. Compare dominant and alternative metaphors.
4. Score reframing depth across multiple layers.
5. Audit power, exclusion, and legitimacy.
6. Translate reframes into scenarios, backcasting targets, and monitoring indicators.

## Core Equations

### CLA Depth Score

`cla_depth = 0.10 × litany_visibility + 0.18 × systemic_explanation + 0.22 × worldview_challenge + 0.20 × myth_metaphor_depth + 0.16 × reframing_potential + 0.08 × power_sensitivity + 0.06 × strategic_relevance`

### Layer Transformation Need

`layer_priority = diagnostic_weight × transformation_need`

### Metaphor Reframing Score

`metaphor_reframe = 0.60 × metaphor_shift_score + 0.40 × ethical_relevance`

### Reframing Depth

`reframing_depth = 0.12 × litany_change + 0.20 × systemic_change + 0.22 × worldview_change + 0.22 × metaphor_change + 0.12 × power_awareness + 0.12 × strategic_coherence`

### Power Risk Score

`power_risk_score = 0.40 × power_risk + 0.30 × exclusion_risk + 0.30 × legitimacy_need`

## Interpretation

The workflow does not claim to automate meaning. It makes layered interpretation, metaphor comparison, power analysis, and scenario translation more organized and auditable.
