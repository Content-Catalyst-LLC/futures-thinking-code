# Data Dictionary

## foresight_capacity_profiles.csv

Synthetic public-sector foresight capacity profiles.

- `scanning_capacity`: Ability to detect weak signals trends disruptions and emerging risks.
- `scenario_capacity`: Ability to create and use multiple plausible futures.
- `decision_uptake`: Degree to which foresight influences real decisions.
- `participation_capacity`: Ability to include communities workers practitioners experts and affected publics.
- `budget_connection`: Connection to public finance prevention budgets resilience investment and lifecycle costing.
- `evaluation_capacity`: Ability to assess foresight quality use impact and public value.
- `institutional_learning`: Ability to update assumptions and routines over time.
- `implementation_authority`: Authority to translate foresight into law regulation procurement programs or delivery.
- `knowledge_infrastructure`: Signal repositories dashboards assumption registers evaluation archives and shared evidence systems.
- `legitimacy`: Public trust transparency contestability and democratic accountability.

## scanning_signals.csv

Weak-signal and horizon-scanning records for public-sector foresight.

## decision_uptake_register.csv

Records describing how foresight enters budget regulation procurement implementation evaluation and participation.

## scenario_cycle_profiles.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative public-sector foresight futures.

## adaptive_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for foresight capacity decision uptake legitimacy and learning over time.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for building public-sector foresight capacity.
