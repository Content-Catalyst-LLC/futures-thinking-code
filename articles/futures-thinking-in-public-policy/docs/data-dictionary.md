# Data Dictionary

## policy_options.csv

Synthetic policy option register for public policy futures analysis.

- `robustness`: Ability to perform acceptably across multiple plausible futures.
- `equity`: Distributional fairness and protection against harm concentration.
- `adaptability`: Ability to revise policy as conditions change.
- `coordination`: Cross-agency and cross-sector coordination quality.
- `legitimacy`: Public trust, transparency, voice, and contestability.
- `implementation_capacity`: Staffing, delivery systems, procurement, legal authority, and operational capacity.
- `political_feasibility`: Near-term feasibility under political and institutional constraints.
- `learning_capacity`: Ability to update assumptions using feedback and evaluation.
- `intergenerational_responsibility`: Degree to which long-term consequences and future publics are considered.

## scenario_profiles.csv

Synthetic scenario profiles for alternative public-policy operating environments.

## institutional_capacity.csv

Institutional readiness records covering detection, learning, coordination, budget alignment, implementation, participation, data infrastructure, and accountability.

## policy_risk_register.csv

Risk records covering short-termism, implementation gaps, legitimacy failure, underfunding, symbolic participation, deferred risk, preparedness fatigue, and policy capture.

## adaptive_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for policy viability, legitimacy, equity, and adaptive learning over time.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for building public-sector foresight capacity and connecting it to policy delivery.
