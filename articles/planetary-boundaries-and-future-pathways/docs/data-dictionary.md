# Data Dictionary

## planetary_pathway_profiles.csv

Synthetic planetary pathway profiles.

- `climate_pressure`: Relative pressure on climate stability.
- `biosphere_pressure`: Relative pressure on biodiversity and ecological resilience.
- `land_pressure`: Relative pressure from land-system change, habitat conversion, and land-use conflict.
- `freshwater_pressure`: Relative pressure on rivers, aquifers, wetlands, soil moisture, and water quality.
- `nutrient_pressure`: Relative pressure from nitrogen and phosphorus cycle disruption.
- `ocean_pressure`: Relative pressure on ocean chemistry, warming, deoxygenation, and marine systems.
- `aerosol_pressure`: Relative pressure from atmospheric aerosols and air-system risk.
- `novel_entity_pressure`: Relative pressure from synthetic chemicals, plastics, persistent pollutants, and industrial compounds.
- `social_foundation_security`: Strength of food, water, shelter, health, energy, education, livelihood, and participation foundations.
- `governance_capacity`: Institutional capacity for monitoring, coordination, law, public finance, and implementation.
- `justice_capacity`: Strength of rights, distributional fairness, participation, and anti-abandonment protections.
- `technology_dependence`: Degree of reliance on future technology, substitution, digital systems, and technical risk management.
- `regeneration_capacity`: Capacity to restore ecosystems, repair systems, reduce pressure, and rebuild living foundations.

## boundary_scenarios.csv

Alternative planetary futures and stress environments.

## pathway_strategy_options.csv

Strategies for mitigation, restoration, circularity, governance, justice, anti-abandonment, and commons coordination.

## planetary_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning and systemic planetary risk indicators.

## planetary_governance_records.csv

Institutional capacity records for planetary governance.

## planetary_pathway_simulations.csv

Simulation parameters for pathway viability, boundary pressure, and adaptive capacity over time.
