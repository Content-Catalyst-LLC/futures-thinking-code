# Data Dictionary

## sustainability_future_profiles.csv

Synthetic sustainability future profiles.

- `ecological_integrity`: Health and regenerative capacity of climate, biodiversity, soil, water, forests, oceans, and ecosystems.
- `social_equity`: Distribution of benefits, burdens, access, wellbeing, and protection.
- `adaptive_capacity`: Ability to learn, adjust, reorganize, and respond under changing conditions.
- `technological_responsibility`: Whether innovation reduces harm without shifting extraction, inequality, or dependency elsewhere.
- `governance_coordination`: Ability to coordinate across sectors, jurisdictions, time horizons, and affected communities.
- `public_finance_capacity`: Ability to fund public goods, adaptation, infrastructure, resilience, and transition.
- `resilience_capacity`: Ability to absorb shocks, preserve essential functions, and recover.
- `justice_legitimacy`: Participation, rights, accountability, legitimacy, and protection for communities most affected by sustainability decisions.
- `degradation_pressure`: Accumulated ecological, social, infrastructure, or institutional stress.

## sustainability_scenarios.csv

Alternative sustainability futures and stress environments.

## transition_strategies.csv

Transition strategies for degradation reduction, managed governance, equitable adaptation, responsible technology, institutional preparedness, regional regeneration, just transition, and transformative sustainability.

## sustainability_risk_indicators.csv

Early-warning indicators for ecological, social, technological, political-economic, and governance risk.

## governance_capacity_records.csv

Governance capacity and democratic legitimacy records.

## transition_pathways.csv

Simulation parameters for sustainability viability, stress exposure, and institutional capacity over time.
