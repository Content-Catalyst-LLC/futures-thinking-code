# Data Dictionary

## financial_system_profiles.csv

Synthetic financial future profiles.

- `leverage`: Use of debt or borrowed exposure that can amplify gains and losses.
- `liquidity_resilience`: Ability to meet obligations and withstand funding or redemption stress.
- `household_security`: Household savings buffers debt affordability income security insurance access and protection from extraction.
- `climate_exposure`: Physical and transition climate risk affecting assets insurance credit municipalities and public finance.
- `nonbank_exposure`: Systemic importance of private credit asset managers shadow banking and market-based finance.
- `digital_run_risk`: Speed of liquidity flight panic payment disruption cyber risk and platform-mediated stress.
- `regulatory_strength`: Capital liquidity consumer protection macroprudential oversight resolution and supervision capacity.
- `public_finance_capacity`: Government ability to invest in public goods resilience adaptation and crisis response.
- `consumer_protection`: Strength of fair lending fee transparency debt protection and anti-extraction rules.
- `productive_investment`: Degree to which finance supports productive capacity rather than speculative extraction.
