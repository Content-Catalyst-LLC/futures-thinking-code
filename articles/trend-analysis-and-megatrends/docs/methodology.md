# Methodology

This companion repository treats trend analysis and megatrend interpretation as structured foresight workflows.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Profile candidate trends and megatrends.
2. Classify ordinary trends, established strategic trends, megatrend candidates, and megatrend-level structural forces.
3. Map interactions among long-term change patterns.
4. Prioritize signals for monitoring.
5. Score assumption vulnerability.
6. Translate trend interpretation into strategy implications.

## Core Equations

### Long-Term Change Profile

`profile = 0.20 × momentum + 0.22 × structural_depth + 0.22 × cross_system_influence - 0.12 × reversibility - 0.14 × uncertainty + 0.10 × distributional_sensitivity`

### Interaction Priority

`interaction_priority = interaction_strength × systemic_risk`

### Signal Watch Score

`watch_score = 0.35 × uncertainty + 0.40 × impact + 0.25 × novelty`

### Indicator Movement Score

`movement_score = (current_value - baseline_value) × confidence`

### Assumption Vulnerability

`vulnerability = exposure × (1 - confidence) × (1 + (1 - reversibility))`

### Strategy Translation Priority

`translation_priority = 0.70 × decision_linkage + 0.30 × review_frequency_weight`

## Interpretation

The workflow does not claim to predict long-term change. It makes trend interpretation, classification, interaction mapping, and strategy translation more transparent and easier to revise.
