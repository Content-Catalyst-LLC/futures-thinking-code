# Methodology

This companion repository treats the ethics of futures thinking as a system shaped by intergenerational responsibility, inclusion, accountability, risk equity, transparency, contestability, precaution, adaptive learning, epistemic pluralism, and public legitimacy.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate profiles, scenarios, strategies, risk indicators, stakeholder distributions, and intergenerational pathways.
2. Score institutional ethical futures profiles.
3. Score scenarios for ethical failure risk and ethical opportunity.
4. Score strategies for ethical value and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for ethical-risk priority.
6. Score stakeholder distribution records for justice-adjusted welfare.
7. Simulate intergenerational benefit, risk, legitimacy, and justice-adjusted scores over time.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Ethical Futures Profile

`profile = 0.13 × intergenerational_responsibility + 0.12 × inclusion + 0.12 × accountability + 0.12 × risk_equity + 0.10 × transparency + 0.10 × contestability + 0.10 × precaution + 0.08 × adaptive_learning + 0.08 × epistemic_pluralism + 0.05 × public_legitimacy`

### Ethical Failure Risk

`failure = technocratic_opacity + risk_inequality + corporate_capture_pressure + security_drift + ai_opacity - participation_depth - accountability_strength - adaptive_learning_capacity`

### Justice-Adjusted Welfare

`justice_adjusted = benefit - risk - inequality_penalty × exposure + moral_weight × protection + voice_weight × participation_bonus`

## Interpretation

Scores are not moral verdicts. They are structured prompts for asking sharper questions about power, inclusion, future generations, risk distribution, accountability, uncertainty, contestability, and adaptive learning.
