# Data Dictionary

## scenario_assumptions.csv

Scenario-level assumptions for stylized complex-system simulations.

- `growth_rate`: Baseline system improvement or development.
- `shock_level`: Disturbance intensity.
- `adaptation_gain`: Initial adaptive capacity contribution.
- `feedback_strength`: Stress feedback that worsens outcomes when system state falls.
- `learning_gain`: Rate at which stress produces adaptive learning.
- `governance_capacity`: Institutional capacity to coordinate response.
- `public_trust`: Legitimacy and cooperation capacity.
- `distributional_pressure`: Unequal exposure and burden.

## strategy_portfolio.csv

Strategy-level assumptions.

- `baseline_boost`: Initial system-state improvement.
- `shock_absorption`: Capacity to absorb or reduce shocks.
- `adaptation_boost`: Strategy contribution to adaptive capacity.
- `equity_weight`: Equity and distributional protection.
- `implementation_complexity`: Cost and implementation burden.
- `governance_dependency`: Degree of dependence on institutional coordination.

## driver_register.csv

Drivers shaping scenario behavior and monitoring priorities.

## monitoring_indicators.csv

Indicators, thresholds, review frequencies, trigger rules, and decision responses.
