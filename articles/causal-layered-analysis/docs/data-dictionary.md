# Data Dictionary

## cla_issues.csv

Synthetic issue register for Causal Layered Analysis.

- `litany_visibility`: How visible the surface issue is in public discourse.
- `systemic_explanation`: Strength of systemic or structural diagnosis.
- `worldview_challenge`: Degree to which dominant assumptions are challenged.
- `myth_metaphor_depth`: Depth of myth/metaphor analysis.
- `reframing_potential`: Potential for alternative future framing.
- `power_sensitivity`: Degree to which power and excluded voices matter.
- `strategic_relevance`: Decision relevance for futures and strategy.

## layer_codes.csv

Layer-coded interpretive statements using CLA's four layers: litany, social causes, worldview, and myth/metaphor.

## metaphor_map.csv

Dominant and alternative metaphors, their effects, shift scores, and ethical relevance.

## reframing_profiles.csv

Old and new layered frames with scores for depth of change across litany, systemic causes, worldview, metaphor, power, and coherence.

## power_audit.csv

Power and legitimacy audit for whose narratives dominate and whose futures may be excluded.

## scenario_translation.csv

Translation of CLA reframes into scenario logic, backcasting targets, and monitoring indicators.
