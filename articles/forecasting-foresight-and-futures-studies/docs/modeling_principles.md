# Modeling Principles

This article distinguishes forecasting, foresight, and futures studies.

## Forecasting

Forecasting estimates likely outcomes using models, data, assumptions, and historical relationships. It is strongest when systems are relatively stable and the time horizon is limited.

## Foresight

Foresight explores plausible futures and tests present decisions under uncertainty. It emphasizes robustness, signal tracking, scenario logic, assumptions, and institutional readiness.

## Futures Studies

Futures studies examines how futures are imagined, contested, governed, and shaped. It adds historical, ethical, cultural, political, and critical dimensions to future-oriented work.

## Modeling Principle

Use forecasting for estimation, foresight for preparedness, and futures studies for critical understanding of future-making.
