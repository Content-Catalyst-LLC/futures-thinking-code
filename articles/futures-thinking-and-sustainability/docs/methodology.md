# Methodology

This companion repository treats futures thinking and sustainability as a multidimensional system involving ecological integrity, social equity, adaptive capacity, technological responsibility, governance coordination, public finance, resilience, justice, degradation pressure, and institutional learning.

## Core Analytical Tasks

1. Validate sustainability profiles, scenarios, transition strategies, risk indicators, governance records, and transition pathways.
2. Score sustainability viability and fragility.
3. Score scenarios for sustainability stress and transition opportunity.
4. Score transition strategies for sustainability gain and implementation readiness.
5. Score risk indicators for sustainability risk priority.
6. Score governance records for institutional capacity and legitimacy.
7. Simulate sustainability viability, stress exposure, and institutional capacity under repeated shocks.
8. Export reproducible tables and a report.

## Core Equations

### Sustainability Viability

`viability = 0.17 × ecological_integrity + 0.15 × social_equity + 0.14 × adaptive_capacity + 0.10 × technological_responsibility + 0.14 × governance_coordination + 0.10 × public_finance_capacity + 0.10 × resilience_capacity + 0.10 × justice_legitimacy - 0.08 × degradation_pressure`

### Sustainability Fragility

`fragility = 0.16 × degradation_pressure + 0.15 × (1 - ecological_integrity) + 0.14 × (1 - social_equity) + 0.13 × (1 - governance_coordination) + 0.12 × (1 - adaptive_capacity) + 0.11 × (1 - public_finance_capacity) + 0.10 × (1 - resilience_capacity) + 0.09 × (1 - justice_legitimacy)`

### Risk Priority

`priority = 0.14 × probability_proxy + 0.18 × severity + 0.17 × irreversibility + 0.16 × systemic_reach + 0.12 × visibility_gap + 0.15 × distributional_harm + 0.08 × (1 - preparedness)`

## Interpretation

Scores are not predictions. They are structured prompts for comparing sustainability futures, surfacing risk, testing transition strategies, and assessing whether institutions can navigate uncertainty without sacrificing ecological integrity, justice, or long-term public value.
