# Data Dictionary

## platform_profiles.csv

Synthetic platform profile register for digital platform futures analysis.

- `network_effect_strength`: Degree to which platform value increases with more participants.
- `data_advantage`: Scale of data accumulation and model/targeting advantage.
- `gatekeeping_power`: Control over market, audience, app, payment, labor, or infrastructure access.
- `lock_in`: Difficulty of leaving the platform or moving data, reputation, relationships, or workflows.
- `interoperability`: Ability to connect, move, or operate across systems.
- `public_accountability`: Transparency, oversight, auditability, enforceability, and public control.
- `user_rights`: Privacy, portability, control, explanation, safety, and contestability.
- `worker_protection`: Pay transparency, appeal rights, bargaining access, and algorithmic-management safeguards.
- `ecological_responsibility`: Attention to energy, devices, logistics, compute, materials, and lifecycle impacts.
- `digital_public_value`: Degree to which the platform supports inclusive, accountable, rights-protecting public value.

## platform_risk_register.csv

Risk records covering public discourse, gatekeeping, labor rights, cloud dependency, public-service exclusion, creator dependence, and AI-mediated opacity.

## accountability_indicators.csv

Accountability records covering transparency, auditability, contestability, enforceability, researcher access, remedy capacity, and public participation.

## platform_scenarios.csv

Scenario profiles for alternative digital platform futures.

## pathway_parameters.csv

Simulation parameters for platform dependency pressure, accountability capacity, and digital public value.

## strategy_options.csv

Strategy portfolio for interoperability, user rights, worker protection, accountability, research access, competition enforcement, public infrastructure, ecological standards, and remedy.
