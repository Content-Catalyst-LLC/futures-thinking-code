# Data Dictionary

## system_domains.csv

Synthetic system-domain register for systems foresight.

- `system_stress`: Current stress or pressure within the system.
- `adaptive_capacity`: Capacity to learn, adapt, coordinate, and respond.
- `public_trust`: Legitimacy, cooperation, and confidence in institutions.
- `interdependence`: Degree to which stress can propagate across connected systems.
- `distributional_vulnerability`: Unequal exposure and unequal adaptive capacity.
- `institutional_fragmentation`: Degree to which authority and responsibility are split.
- `structural_lock_in`: Degree to which infrastructure, rules, or incentives preserve the current trajectory.

## feedback_loops.csv

Feedback-loop register describing reinforcing or balancing loops.

## leverage_points.csv

Candidate structural interventions scored by intervention depth, reach, feasibility, legitimacy, equity, and readiness.

## signals_pressure.csv

Signal register for emerging pressure, weak signals, accountability failures, and structural stress.

## strategy_pathways.csv

Strategy pathways for simulated structural pressure over time.

## monitoring_triggers.csv

Indicators, thresholds, review frequencies, trigger rules, and decision responses.
